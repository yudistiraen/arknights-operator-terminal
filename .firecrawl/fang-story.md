A young [Kazimierzian](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Kazimierz "Kazimierz") [Kuranta](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Kuranta "Kuranta") raised in [Columbia](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Columbia "Columbia"), Fang decided to follow many Kazimierzian immigrants and move to the country when she was 10 years old. Inspired by the Columbian Police Force's ideals of justice and equality, she later became a police cadet in [Tkaronto](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Tkaronto "Tkaronto") who worked together with [Kroos](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Kroos "Kroos") and [Beagle](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Beagle "Beagle"), after the three of whom earned the highest grades in the Tkaronto Police Academy's history.

When a [Catastrophe](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Catastrophe "Catastrophe") titled "Blackwasp" hit the city, however, Fang and her friends risked their lives to save as many lives as possible in Tkaronto's [Infected](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Infected "Infected") district —Plate #4, contracting [Oripathy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oripathy "Oripathy") in the process. She then joined [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island") together with her colleagues to escape discrimination as Infected within her police station and ultimately overcoming her grief for Plate #4's residents' deaths. In R.I., she is putting her police training and speed to good use as a vanguard and the leader of [Op Reserve Team A1](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Op_Reserve_A1 "Op Reserve A1").

### _Operations Preparation Detachment A1_\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Fang/Story?action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Operations Preparation Detachment A1")\]

### Stories of Afternoon\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Fang/Story?action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Stories of Afternoon")\]

Reserve Team A1 has just finished their tests, with Fang passing hers. Instructor Grace assures them that the tests were not designed to be easy, as there is a large skill gap between trainees and instructors. Fang, Beagle, and Kroos listen as Grace reminisces that [Dobermann](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Dobermann "Dobermann") was still like a real soldier during the early days of Rhodes Island and that she had always cared about people. Fang offers to take the test results to Dobermann for Grace, and Beagle comes along with her.

Curious about Dobermann's past, Fang offers to help her clean her room. The offer surprises Dobermann, who had always thought of them as being fearful of her. As they help her sort through the multitude of items in her room, the two ask questions about her past. Dobermann then explains to them the three factions in Bolívar and that she was once a colonel serving the autonomous government.

When Dobermann asks the two what they think is the defining characteristic of a soldier, Fang first answers combat proficiency and then numbers, the latter coming close to the answer. Dobermann points out that it is obedience to the chain of command, which is why the correct orders must be issued. She reveals that she defected to the resistance but eventually left, and was pleasantly surprised when she moved to Rhodes Island. Although the two trainees fail to fully grasp her philosophies, Dobermann tells them to appreciate Rhodes Island. The two then hurriedly leave when they realize it is already lunchtime.[\[1\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Fang/Story#cite_note-1)

### Kroos: Apples\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Fang/Story?action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Kroos: Apples")\]

Fang looks for Kroos, who has slipped away. She assures [Beagle](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Beagle "Beagle") that she is not mad, simply frustrated that she cannot keep Kroos working whenever the [Cautus](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Cautus "Cautus") wants to slack off. She then spots Kroos and chases after her, nearly discovering the camouflaged Kroos. In the cafeteria, Beagle apologizes for going after the wrong person, and Fang replies [Leonhardt](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Leonhardt "Leonhardt") and Kroos are still different despite both being Cautuses. Fang says they can ask [Meteor](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Meteor "Meteor") or [Gitano](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Gitano "Gitano") for help in finding Kroos. She reiterates she is not mad, and that Kroos mentioned bringing back a present since today is the anniversary of Fang becoming their leader.

The two then spot Kroos in a hurry and follow her to [Mayer](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Mayer "Mayer")'s workshop. Seeing that Kroos is actually busy, Fang says they can go back to their room to clean up Kroos's mess and get something to eat. Later that night, after Kroos returns, Fang thanks her for the muffins and explains to Beagle that she was not going to reprimand Kroos, who can take a break as long as she tells Fang first. The Kuranta sighs and wishes Kroos would treat her normally, then reminds Beagle to wake Kroos up, as they have training with [Dobermann](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Dobermann "Dobermann") in the morning. She then tells Beagle good night.[\[2\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Fang/Story#cite_note-2)
