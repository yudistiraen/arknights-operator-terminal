A mobile [Banshee](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Banshee "Banshee") witchcraft platform developed as part of a collaboration between [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island") and the Convallis of the Banshees, the first and only R&V Model unit designated PhonoR-0 functions more like a self-propelled witchcraft altar than a robot, operated by an A.I. that is more intelligent than other robots fielded by R.I. Even though its specifications are far below [Terran](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Terra "Terra") standards, PhonoR-0 is nonetheless capable of providing assistance in the battlefield and acting as an attendant of [Logos](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Logos "Logos") outside of combat.
