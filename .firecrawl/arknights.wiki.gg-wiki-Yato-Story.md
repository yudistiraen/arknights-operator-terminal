**Yato** is an [Oni](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oni "Oni") combat personnel of [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island") part of [Op A4](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Op_A4 "Op A4") along with [Noir Corne](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Noir_Corne "Noir Corne") and [Rangers](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rangers "Rangers"). All about her past is that she was once an assassin hailing from a [Higashinese](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Higashi "Higashi") village deep in the mountains. Her village was known for training some of the most powerful mercenaries around, of which her parents were amongst the best. With such a lifestyle forged from the very begining, Yato eventually left her hometown and accepted her first contract from a tall, green-haired woman, and later conscripted during the Higashi civil war where she defeated Noir Corne on a particular mission, thogh Yato prefers to keep this past in low profile. The two eventually found their way after a hilarious, "romantic" moment when they decided to desert the army after sparing their target and flee the war-torn country, until they somehow ended in [Kazdel](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Kazdel "Kazdel") and were arrested by [Babel](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Babel "Babel").

In an act of mercy, however, [Theresa](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Theresa "Theresa") praised Yato's combat skills and offered her becoming an Operator. From that point onwards hse acted as a silent guardian for her Babelite comrades all the way until Theresa's assasination, when she was sent to provide support at the outskits. But as bitter as it was, Yato didn't mourn Her Highness' passing. Instead, she naturally joined Rhodes Island alongside Noir Corne.

Aside from her years of combat experience both during the war and in R.I., Yato is known for her light-sensitive skin that makes hard to withstand sunlight. As a matter of fact, she has been wearing her mask all the time. While many are curious about her reason, only Noir Corne understands her. Nevertheless, there will be a day when Yato finally takes off her mask and braves herself against her own weakness.
