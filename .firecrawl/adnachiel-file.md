| − **Profile** |
| --- |
| Adnachiel is a citizen of [Laterano](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Laterano "Laterano") and enjoys rights one through thirteen afforded to all citizens of Laterano. Before his arrival at [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island"), he worked as a freelancer. He received excellent results on a battery of tests and was assigned to Rhodes Island Op Reserve Team A4.<br>He has demonstrated great potential in the areas of rapid response, marksmanship, and support operations. |

| + **Clinical Analysis** <br>Have at least 25% Trust with Adnachiel |
| --- |
| Medical tests have shown the outlines of his internal organs to be indistinct due to abnormal shadows. [Originium](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Originium "Originium") granules have been detected in his circulatory system, confirming him to be infected with [Oripathy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oripathy "Oripathy").<br>**\[Cell-Originium Assimilation\]** 9%<br>He presents no obvious external symptoms of Oripathy.<br>**\[Blood Originium-Crystal Density\]** 0.26u/L<br>Degree of infection is minor and has not displayed any significant physical symptoms. |

| + **Archive File 1** <br>Have at least 50% Trust with Adnachiel |
| --- |
| Operators familiar with Adnachiel tend to use one word to describe the angel: bright. No matter how complicated the subject or advanced the technology involved, he seems to have no trouble mastering it in a short period of time.<br>In a show of humility, Adnachiel stands helpless in the face of loftier, theoretical topics, though everyone assumes he's just being modest. After all, as soft spoken and kind as he is, Adnachiel gives off the impression of being a clever and considerate young man. After meeting him, most operators come to understand his observant and sensitive personality. He always speaks to the true nature of things, but the scope of his theories is limited to that over which he has immediate control. Through his analyses, he can even predict the short-term future of his targets. He consistently rejects the titles of both "prophet" and "genius pretty boy," but rumors about him continue to proliferate. |

| + **Archive File 2** <br>Have at least 100% Trust with Adnachiel |
| --- |
| When he has free time between missions, Adnachiel often goes to the Rhodes Island Maintenance and Repair Workshop. Working as an intern assistant, Adnachiel's wrists are flexible and his attention to detail is meticulous. He has demonstrated an insatiable curiosity about Rhodes Island's equipment. |

| + **Archive File 3** <br>Have at least 150% Trust with Adnachiel |
| --- |
| Adnachiel has not yet completed the Laterano Firearms Usage Agreement. His most trusted piece of equipment is his personally modified crossbow (Laterano crossbows are very different from the metal crossbows mass produced by [Raythean](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Raythean "Raythean")). As he is not sufficiently physically strong, Adnachiel's crossbow has a pedal on the front used to help pull back the string with his foot. |
