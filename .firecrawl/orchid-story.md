Orchid once worked as an editor for a [Columbian](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Columbia "Columbia") fashion magazine before her sudden infection. As a matter of fact, she always wears an elaborate and expensive designer outfit despite being a field operative. She originally joined [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island") as a desk worker, but now Orchid acts as the captain of [Reserve Op Team A6](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Op_Reserve_A6 "Op Reserve A6") where she provides battlefield support to others with her Originium Arts. She has the reputation of being the "nagging mother" of her team since Orchid has to deal with her teammates' eccentricities all the time.

### Stories of Afternoon\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Orchid/Story?action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Stories of Afternoon")\]

During a conversation with a logistics operator, [Orchid](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Orchid "Orchid") recalls when she first arrived at Rhodes Island and chose a combat role just to avoid sitting in an office. She tells him that she simply fell into the position, and he responds by inviting her to join the logistics department, as combat operators with a civilian background and a year of experience are eligible to apply. As [Popukar](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Popukar "Popukar") appears and they leave, the logistics operator reminds Orchid to think it over. Distressed by the thought of returning to an office job, Orchid asks Popukar how she joined Rhodes Island. Popukar reveals she worked at a lumberyard before being found by [Ansel](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ansel "Ansel") and [Kal'tsit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Kal%27tsit "Kal'tsit"), and a horrified Orchid hugs the innocent girl.

[Catapult](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Catapult "Catapult") appears and mentions that she just finished working out with Midnight, who is currently training with Spot. The news surprises Orchid. Nevertheless, Orchid reminds Catapult not to cause trouble. Afterward, Orchid and Popukar find Spot and Midnight sparring in the training room. Orchid is indignant that Midnight has a session outside of his daily routine without telling her, but he smoothly talks his way out of it. He explains his philosophy of treating all his clients equally and tells Orchid that she shouldn't let her civilian background shackle her. They then move to the cafeteria, where Midnight treats them to a meal while answering Spot's earlier question about how he became an operator. He then asks Orchid what kind of life she wants to lead at Rhodes Island, and she thanks him for his advice. Orchid and Popukar leave the cafeteria, and Orchid tells Popukar not to go to Catapult's room as it is already late, while the [Cautus](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Cautus "Cautus") girl expresses relief that Orchid seems happier than before. The two then come across Sora, who introduces herself and asks about Orchid's expertise in fashion design.

Later, Orchid decides to reject the logistics department's offer and signs up for fitness training. She is interrupted by Catapult, who exclaims that Midnight is currently sparring with Popukar. When Catapult insists that she hasn't caused any trouble lately and Orchid says she believes her, Catapult remarks that Orchid seems different. As she watches Catapult rush off, eager to see [Midnight](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Midnight "Midnight") lose, Orchid muses about the troubles she might find after choosing to look for them.[\[1\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Orchid/Story#cite_note-1)

### Rewinding Breeze\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Orchid/Story?action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Rewinding Breeze")\]

Orchid is the one who told [Popukar](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Popukar "Popukar") about the story of the [Forte](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Forte "Forte") inventor and the wax figure. When asked by Popukar where the wax bride went, she said she was stuck in the tower after it collapsed. Near the end of the storytelling session, Orchid appears and calls for Popukar, as it is her bedtime.[\[2\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Orchid/Story#cite_note-2)

### Midnight: Letter from Home\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Orchid/Story?action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Midnight: Letter from Home")\]

[Spot](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Spot "Spot") uses [Midnight](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Midnight "Midnight")'s phone to call Orchid, who threatens to cut Midnight's overtime pay if he keeps talking nonsense. The three then discuss the details of the mission, which involves rescuing a kidnapped boy from the local gangsters. Aware of urgent message from [Higashi](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Higashi "Higashi"), Orchid tells Midnight that he can switch with [Catapult](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Catapult "Catapult") if necessary. Midnight declines, stating that he will not let trivial matters from home interfere with his work. After the mission is finished, Midnight and Spot report to Orchid, who indignantly wonders if they should have just let the police arrest Jeremy for swindling money from his parents. Midnight reassures her that Jeremy won't act out again. Despite Midnight's usual flirtatiousness, Orchid tells him that she and Spot are willing to listen to him. Moved, Midnight offers to hug them, but is immediately told off before he can embrace Spot. He tells them his past. When Orchid questions him further, he reveals that he never told his parents about his Oripathy, but arranged for someone to deliver money using the savings he left behind in Higashi. Midnight starts to ramble to Orchid, and becomes lost in his musings.[\[3\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Orchid/Story#cite_note-3)

### Istina: Let's Go, Let's Go\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Orchid/Story?action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Istina: Let's Go, Let's Go")\]

On the day of the food festival, Orchid and the other operators visit the booth run by the [U.S.S.G.](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ursus_Student_Self-Governing_Group "Ursus Student Self-Governing Group") girls. Orchid scolds [Catapult](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Catapult "Catapult") when the girl offers to teach [Popukar](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Popukar "Popukar") how to catch a pastry in her mouth.[\[4\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Orchid/Story#cite_note-4)

### Spot: Face of the Hero\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Orchid/Story?action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Spot: Face of the Hero")\]

Op Reserve A6 heads to the cafeteria together for a party. [Catapult](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Catapult "Catapult") laughs and boasts about giving the drills everything she had, but when asked by Orchid, she admits to only putting in seventy to eighty percent effort. Orchid remains quiet when [Midnight](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Midnight "Midnight") nominates himself as the team member with the best battle performance, and tells him to shut up when he bemoans their cruelty. Orchid tells him to shut up again when he chimes in on Popukar and Catapult's praise of Spot, and she points out Spot's terrible mood. Spot realizes that Popukar had told them about the comic about Bornu the soldier. Noticing the sullen air, Orchid tells Popukar not to bring up the topic for the moment, and goes along when the others try to steer the conversation back to the party. Popukar apologizes to Spot, who reassures her that he is just unsure how to talk about his past. Orchid looks concerned when Popukar asks if she can tell everyone about Spot's story. Spot shares his past exploits with his teammates and talks about the comic. She tries to dissuade him from the self-pity, but agrees with the notion that he didn't want Popukar to tell others as heroics like that are not his style. Orchid then reassures the confused Popukar that she will understand after growing up. Spot then admits that they are the people he trusts the most, and they have a toast for him. They spend hours trying to talk Popukar out of her idea of holding a medal ceremony, though she later makes Spot a hero certificate that they can all sign.[\[5\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Orchid/Story#cite_note-5)

### Orchid: Incomplete\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Orchid/Story?action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: Orchid: Incomplete")\]

On a Monday morning, Orchid receives a request from Jerry to make a wedding dress. He plans to marry Penny, who [Warfarin](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Warfarin "Warfarin") said might not make it through the month. Although hesitant, Orchid accepts the request and takes the sickly girl's measurements on Tuesday. Penny thanks Orchid and — after asking Jerry to get her a bowl of vegetable soup — inquires about Orchid's designs. Orchid confesses that she used to work in design but left the profession after her instructor commented that her designs lacked spirit. In turn, Penny admits that she wants Orchid to design her wedding dress.

In her room, Orchid ponders the materials and design for the dress before going to find inspiration. The next day, she interrogates Jerry about his and Penny's meeting in Siesta and then calls [Ceylon](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ceylon "Ceylon") to ask for pictures and videos of the city. On Thursday, she asks Penny about their first date and then requests to pick up some hydrangeas from [Perfumer](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Perfumer "Perfumer"). After hearing Penny's story, Perfumer tells Orchid she can pick up the flowers the next day, despite needing them for her own perfumes. Perfumer, worried Orchid might exhaust herself, reminds her to get some rest. On Friday, Orchid discovers the couple's hobby of papercutting and asks Bibeak about adding such elements to the laces.

After several days, the dress is almost complete. Orchid praises the darling she has made and hopes Penny will like it too. On Monday, she shows the dress to Penny. Despite her love for the beautiful garment, Penny apologizes to Orchid and begs her not to finish it. She selfishly admits that she cannot marry Jerry and that she never expected Orchid to complete it. Penny explains that she does not want to bind Jerry with their vows and hopes he can find someone else. Later that night, Orchid mourns for her creation as she tears it apart with a pair of scissors.

Two weeks later, over a glass of whiskey, she recounts the story to an Operator from Human Resources. Orchid mentions that Jerry was not angry when she told him the dress could not be finished. She adds that Penny died on her supposed wedding day and that she did not attend the funeral. Jerry had visited her that morning and given her a pair of scissors. Now, she drowns her sorrows, regretting that she didn't encourage Penny to go ahead with the wedding. She admits that all she did was run away as the situation reminded her of her own [Oripathy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oripathy "Oripathy"). She raises a toast to Jerry and Penny, to dreams and failure, to the annoying design instructor she once had, and to life and death.[\[6\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Orchid/Story#cite_note-6)
