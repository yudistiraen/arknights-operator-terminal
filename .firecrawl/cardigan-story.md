The young [Leithanian](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Leithanian "Leithanian") [Perro](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Perro "Perro") **Merry** is a former member of the Leithanien Bobsled Patrol Team, which she joined to make up for her failure to become a skier. At some point, Merry met [Steward](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Steward "Steward") and [Adnachiel](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Adnachiel "Adnachiel") and quickly befriended them. However, the three got caught in a snowstorm while hiking, which caused both Steward and Adnachiel to suffer frostbite and even worsened their [Oripathy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oripathy "Oripathy"). After five hours of desperate searching for medical aid, Merry finally found [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island") requesting to become an operator in exchange for the duo's treatment. She picks up the codename "Cardigan" and works as a member of [Op Reserve A4](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Op_Reserve_A4 "Op Reserve A4"), where she acts as a defender. However, Cardigan is also infamous for her hyperactive behavior, which has caused many problems for her teammates now and then.

### Adnachiel: Reserve Prophet\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Cardigan/Story?action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Adnachiel: Reserve Prophet")\]

Cardigan helps solve the problem in Ashford by rushing to the branch offices in the adjacent counties of Dovel and Hafilax. Cardigan claims that they only trust [Adnachiel](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Adnachiel "Adnachiel") because they have yet to see the evil underneath him, which [Ansel](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ansel "Ansel") comments is simply him tricking her into paying his bills once in a while, and then she swears to make him pay for her meals for a full week. She makes it in time and successfully brings their representatives to convince the Viscount, arguing that the other branches will bring in manpower and resources to build a new hospital on the undeveloped plate.

The team later visits Adnachiel in prison. Cardigan calls his name, causing him to exclaim he was about to die of fright. He questions paying off a whole week of meals for her, to which Cardigan has [Melantha](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Melantha "Melantha") help her pressure him to keep him from escaping. He then mourns the naive Cardigan he used to know, to which she claims has become harder to fool thanks to a certain prophet. Cardigan notes his humility is similar to the Doctor's, and then asks if they should get something to eat. Just before they leave, a Lateran messenger steps in. As Adnachiel explains his reason for returning to Laterano, Cardigan answers that reserve Operators are those who uphold Rhodes Island's mission and commit themselves to it, and he finishes by saying that he wants to understand what is happening in Terra. Cardigan, along with the others sans Ansel, finds his seriousness as strange, causing Adnachiel to regret saying so much. At Cardigan's request, he promises to bring back new Lateran desserts.[\[1\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Cardigan/Story#cite_note-1)
