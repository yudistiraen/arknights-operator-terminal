| − **Profile** |
| --- |
| Captain of [Op Reserve Team A1](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Op_Reserve_A1 "Op Reserve A1"). She has prior experience working with a [Columbian](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Columbia "Columbia") [garrison](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Tkaronto "Tkaronto"), and has proven herself to be outstanding in combat. After joining [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island"), she had no problem adjusting to her current work. She joined the Operation Planning Group with its original members, [Beagle](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Beagle "Beagle") and [Kroos](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Kroos "Kroos"), and has worked hard to become its leading [Operator](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Operator "Operator"). |

| + **Clinical Analysis** <br>Have at least 25% Trust with Fang (the Fire-Sharpened) |
| --- |
| Imaging tests have shown the outlines of her internal organs to be indistinct due to abnormal shadows. [Originium](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Originium "Originium") granules have been detected in her circulatory system, confirming her to be infected with [Oripathy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oripathy "Oripathy").<br>**\[Cell-Originium Fusion Rate\] 7.4%**<br>She presents no obvious external symptoms of Oripathy.<br>**\[Blood Crystal Density\] 0.2 u/L**<br>Degree of infection is minor and physical symptoms are not yet present. |

| + **Archive File 1** <br>Have at least 50% Trust with Fang (the Fire-Sharpened) |
| --- |
| Fang shows off a [Kuranta](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Kuranta "Kuranta")'s natural dominance in battle by always holding the most advantageous position at the critical moment. To strike a balance between power and control, Fang prefer to use a spear, which she applies with versatility in any situation. Fang acts impulsively and springs into action before others even finish talking. |

| + **Archive File 2** <br>Have at least 100% Trust with Fang (the Fire-Sharpened) |
| --- |
| Immigrants from many nations came to the outskirts of Columbia. Fang, Kroos, and Beagle all met there after moving from their respective homelands. After realizing their common goals, they joined the city guard and underwent basic training together. The humorless Fang, the gentle Beagle, and the laid-back Kroos possess wildly different personalities yet rely on one another for support. |

| + **Archive File 3** <br>Have at least 150% Trust with Fang (the Fire-Sharpened) |
| --- |
| [Before an election day, a Catastrophe struck the outskirts of Columbia](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/A1_Operations_Preparation_Detachment "A1 Operations Preparation Detachment"). In their efforts to aid others in escaping, all three Operators were infected with Oripathy. Fang suffered discrimination due to her condition and fell into deep despair. Kroos rallied the three of them and together they went to Rhodes Island. |

| + **Archive File 4** <br>Have 200% Trust with Fang (the Fire-Sharpened) |
| --- |
| After arriving at Rhodes Island, Fang and her two companions as well as [Hibiscus](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Hibiscus "Hibiscus") and [Lava](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lava "Lava") were all trained by [Dobermann](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Dobermann "Dobermann"). The training was extraordinarily intense, and Kroos and Hibiscus seized every opportunity they could to goof off. Even Fang as their captain couldn't do anything to control them. Currently, Fang is training herself to be as intense and strict as her teacher, Dobermann. However, if the giddy expression on her face as she eats fruit is any indication, she's got a long way to go. |
