A former [Sarkaz](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Sarkaz "Sarkaz") host from [Higashi](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Higashi "Higashi"), **Yoru**[\[3\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Midnight/Story#cite_note-oprec-3)[\[note 1\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Midnight/Story#cite_note-4) used to be very popular among the ladies — as a host — back in the day, earning him monikers like "the Demon King of Higashi". After contracting [Oripathy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oripathy "Oripathy") thanks to a jealous rival host's machinations, he left the nightlife and went to [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island") for treatment, eventually becoming a combat [operator](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Operator "Operator"). As a member of [Op Reserve A6](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Op_Reserve_A6 "Op Reserve A6") under the codename "Midnight", Yoru has proved himself to be a surprisingly competent combatant through his impressive swordsmanship skills. Amusingly, Yoru's charm has little effect on his fellow R.I. operators, especially not his team captain [Orchid](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Orchid "Orchid"). However, despite his outward suave and laid-back attitude, he genuinely cares for others more than he lets on. His mother has been asking him to return home and visit his sick father, despite his father saying otherwise.[\[3\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Midnight/Story#cite_note-oprec-3)

## Plot\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Midnight/Story?action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Plot")\]

### Stories of Afternoon\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Midnight/Story?action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Stories of Afternoon")\]

Midnight trains with [Catapult](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Catapult "Catapult"), then with Spot. He loses to Spot just as [Orchid](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Orchid "Orchid") and [Popukar](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Popukar "Popukar") appear. Orchid is indignant that Midnight has a session outside of his daily routine without telling her, but he smoothly talks his way out of it. He explains his philosophy of treating all his clients equally and tells Orchid that she shouldn't let her civilian background shackle her.

They then move to the cafeteria, where Midnight treats them to a meal while answering Spot's earlier question about how he became an operator. He was at the top of his industry as an escort, until one day he saw a bright newcomer whom he thought would surpass him. Driven by a hidden envy, the newcomer attempted to kill him once, then managed to have an agent infect him with [Oripathy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oripathy "Oripathy") on the second try. He asks Orchid what kind of life she wants to lead at Rhodes Island, and she thanks him for his advice. After Orchid and Popukar leave, Spot and Midnight continue their conversation, in which Midnight reveals that his most important trick is to always tell the truth.

Later, Midnight has a spar with Popukar and is implied to be losing.[\[4\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Midnight/Story#cite_note-5)

### Midnight: Letter From Home\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Midnight/Story?action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Midnight: Letter From Home")\]

On the night of a mission, [Spot](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Spot "Spot") knocks on the bathroom door, urging Midnight to hurry up. While Midnight is still showering, his phone rings, and Spot answers it on his behalf. A voice message in Higashinese, transferred by a Rhodes Island messenger, begins to play. In it, Midnight's mother expresses her worries and informs that his father's health has worsened. She begs "Yoru" to contact them despite his father saying otherwise. The voice message is paused midway as Midnight leaves the bathroom, his hair still dripping wet. He states that he can't bear to let his partner wait out in the cold. Midnight's phone is used by Spot to call [Orchid](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Orchid "Orchid"), who threatens to cut Midnight's overtime pay if he keeps talking nonsense. The three then discuss the details of the mission, which involves rescuing a kidnapped boy from the local gangsters. Aware of the urgent message from Higashi, Orchid tells Midnight that he can switch with [Catapult](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Catapult "Catapult") if necessary. Midnight declines, stating that he will not let trivial matters from home interfere with his work. He hangs up the phone and reassures Spot in his usual flamboyant manner.

With his fabulous acting, Midnight tricks a female friend of the missing boy, Jeremy, into thinking they are local gangsters. Frightened, she reveals Jeremy's location. They soon find him drunk in a bar, angrily venting about how his father forces him to study when all he wants is to make music. Midnight's exaggerated praise prompts Jeremy to start playing his guitar — much to the agony of everyone's ears. Other patrons begin to complain. When Midnight bluntly admits how terrible the performance was, Jeremy throws his guitar in frustration. Midnight picks it up and strums a few chords himself. He tells Jeremy that pursuing one's dreams always comes at a price. Jeremy misinterprets this advice as about money and begins rambling about the gangs he's gotten involved with. In response, Midnight tosses a glass of wine at him to sober him up. After a brief exchange about Jeremy's concerned parents, Midnight reveals his identity as a Rhodes Island Operator. This immediately alarms Jeremy, who assumes something has happened to his mother and rushes out of the bar.

Back at Rhodes Island, Midnight and Spot report to Orchid, who indignantly wonders if they should have just let the police arrest Jeremy for swindling money from his parents. Midnight reassures her that Jeremy won't act out again, while Spot mentions that the boy cried after hearing about Midnight's past. Despite Midnight's usual flirtatiousness, Orchid tells him that she and Spot, who has yet to leave the room, are willing to listen to him. Moved, Midnight offers to hug them, but is immediately told off before he can embrace Spot. Spot recalls the story Midnight told Jeremy: how his father once beat him with a family heirloom sword out in the snow, but Midnight still left home and eventually rose to the top of his field. Midnight continues from where he left off, explaining how he sent money home and received letters from his mother, while his father refused all contact. When Orchid questions him further, he reveals that he never told his parents about his Oripathy, but arranged for someone to deliver money using the savings he left behind in Higashi. He muses whether someone who hasn't been home for nearly two decades still has a reason to return. Spot asks him if he is scared, to which Midnight initially responds with his usual attitude, but then admits that there are problems he is not willing to face. He starts to ramble to Orchid, and then spends the night drinking at the pub with Spot.

As the sun begins to rise, Midnight invites Spot for another round of drinks, an invitation Spot declines as he believes that Midnight will be going on a long trip soon. Midnight's terminal plays the message again, and Midnight tells an annoyed Spot in Higashinese that he will see him tomorrow. Alone in his room, he replays the voice message. His mother tells him his father survived so long because he wishes to see him again, and that both of them are sorry. Caught between disbelief and relief, Midnight laughs, wondering why he had been so afraid of going home. He plans to write back before buying a ticket to Higashi.[\[3\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Midnight/Story#cite_note-oprec-3)

### Spot: Face of the Hero\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Midnight/Story?action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Spot: Face of the Hero")\]

Op Reserve A6 heads to the cafeteria together for a party. [Spot](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Spot "Spot") stays quiet when Midnight nominates himself as the team member with the best battle performance, seemingly distracted by the events of the previous day. Midnight bemoans their cruelty, then shrugs elegantly when Orchid tells him to shut up. He agrees with Catapult and Popukar that Spot had performed the best against [Dobermann](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Dobermann "Dobermann"), but Orchid tells him to shut up again, pointing out Spot's terrible mood. Spot realizes that Popukar had told them about the comic. Noticing the sullen air, Midnight goes along with Catapult's attempt to change the topic. Popukar apologizes to Spot, who reassures her that he is just unsure how to talk about his past. He tells Midnight to say something, and Midnight's flamboyant reply of having an oasis of knowledge, or a brain full of water according to Spot, only confuses Popukar even more.

Spot shares his past exploits with his teammates and talks about the comic. Midnight tries to tell Spot that wondering about a path where he did not desert is of no use, but his story is cut short as Spot correctly guesses it to be about a Higashinese friend of his who joined the military and couldn't fit in. Midnight lets out a laugh at the accurate guess. Popukar asks Spot if he is sick, although Midnight tells her that he is simply embarrassed to say words of admiration for them. After a moment of silence, Spot invites Midnight to spar the next day, to which Midnight readily agrees. Spot then admits that they are the people he trusts the most, and they have a toast for him. Popukar insists that he is a hero and claims she will hold a medal ceremony for him and invite the team members and her friends. They spend hours trying to talk her out of the idea.

Midnight later visits Spot, bringing him a hero certificate made by Popukar that they can all sign. Spot does not sign it and instead frames it on his nightstand. Midnight chides him, prompting Spot to comment that he is just worried about Orchid complaining if Popukar makes a fuss about the unsigned certificate, and admits that he is not a hero. The confession silences Midnight for a moment, but his boast of being overwhelmed at successfully melting Spot's heart makes Spot change his mind. He claims that he can be A6's hero by ensuring Midnight gets beaten up due to flirting with a bad-tempered girl during their next mission. The two then share a laugh.[\[5\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Midnight/Story#cite_note-6)
