| − **Profile** |
| --- |
| Born in Londinium in [Victoria](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Victoria "Victoria"), Lava is the younger twin sister of her fellow [Operator](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Operator "Operator"), [Hibiscus](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Hibiscus "Hibiscus"). After contracting [Oripathy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oripathy "Oripathy"), she came to [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island") for treatment. She found the idea of saving lives appealing, and so volunteered for training. |

| + **Clinical Analysis** <br>Have at least 25% Trust with Lava (the Purgatory) |
| --- |
| Imaging tests have shown the outlines of her internal organs to be indistinct due to abnormal shadows. [Originium](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Originium "Originium") granules have been detected in her circulatory system, confirming her to be infected with [Oripathy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oripathy "Oripathy").<br>**\[Cell-Originium Assimilation\]** 9.4%<br>She presents no obvious external symptoms of Oripathy.<br>**\[Blood Originium-Crystal Density\]** 0.25 u/L<br>Degree of infection is minor and has not had any physical impact. |

| + **Originium Arts Rating** |
| --- |
| Under the guidance of her [Originium Arts](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Originium_Arts "Originium Arts") instructor, Lava learned how to control basic Arts in less than a month despite never touching Originium before. Her methods are still rudimentary, and at present she can still only use simple flame conversions, but her stability and learning speed are astounding. If she trains for a long period of time, she may be able to reach a very high level. |

| + **Archive File 1** <br>Have at least 50% Trust with Lava (the Purgatory) |
| --- |
| Unlike other stray children hiding in dark alleys, Lava was born into a relatively normal middle-class Victorian family. Lava believes that one must personally put in effort to help others, rather than stand at a distance. When she was young, she enjoyed working with the poor and the orphaned. She was very aware how much pain they suffered, which planted in her heart the seeds of compassion and justice. |

| + **Archive File 2** <br>Have at least 100% Trust with Lava (the Purgatory) |
| --- |
| Lava is tough and ignores the advice of others when she moves into action. In fact, she is gentle and childlike at heart but prefers not to admit it to herself. Whenever anyone brings this up, she immediately changes the subject and tries to play it cool. |
