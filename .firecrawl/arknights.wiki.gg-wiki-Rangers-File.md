| Rangers serves on [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island") [Op Reserve Team A4](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Op_A4 "Op A4"). A longtime veteran, he has traveled to many different places prior to serving in Rhodes Island's reserves. An expert archer, he still retains the characteristic agility and vigor of the [Savras](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Savra "Savra") despite his advanced age. |
