Modified from a [Raythean](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Raythean "Raythean") Exister-S62 six-wheeled battlefield triage robot, Lancet-2 plays a role in both medical support and [Originium](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Originium "Originium") cleanup for Rhodes Island with a feminine, self-aware A.I. seemingly programmed to adore her creator, [Closure](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Closure "Closure"), while not performing her tasks. Her rounded design makes her look cute, and although she can be depressing due to her heightened pessimism and self-deprecation, Lancet-2 is rather well-liked.
