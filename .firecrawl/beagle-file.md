| − **Profile** |
| --- |
| A frontline [Operator](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Operator "Operator") on [Op Reserve Team A1](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Op_Reserve_A1 "Op Reserve A1"). Beagle originally came to [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island") with [Fang](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Fang "Fang") and [Kroos](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Kroos "Kroos"). Though immature in some respects, she has gradually displayed professionalism, resilience, and extreme talent. As a result, she has earned the recognition of others as a reliable Operator. |

| + **Clinical Analysis** <br>Have at least 25% Trust with Beagle |
| --- |
| Imaging tests have shown the outlines of her internal organs to be indistinct due to abnormal shadows. [Originium](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Originium "Originium") granules have been detected in her circulatory system, confirming her to be infected with [Oripathy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oripathy "Oripathy").<br>**\[Cell-Originium Assimilation\]** 8%<br>She presents no obvious external symptoms of Oripathy.<br>**\[Blood Originium-Crystal Density\]** 0.21u/L<br>Degree of infection is not yet severe and has not yet brought about a significant physical impact. |

| + **Archive File 1** <br>Have at least 50% Trust with Beagle |
| --- |
| Though she lacks flashy skills or the capacity for Arts, Beagle uses her endurance to establish a strong forward defense for her teammates. Though a bit clumsy, she believes that everything will work out as long as she continues to press forward tenaciously. So far, she has only been proven right in this regard. |

| + **Archive File 2** <br>Have at least 100% Trust with Beagle |
| --- |
| Her time in a [Columbia](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Columbia "Columbia") [outskirts garrison](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Tkaronto "Tkaronto") was one of the most difficult periods of her life, but also among the most precious. Beagle, along with her close companions Fang and Kroos, worked tirelessly to close the gap between them and the official team members. However, Beagle fell into a deep bout of self-loathing after being infected with Oripathy. She felt that she had become a burden on Fang and Kroos, and that it was her fault they lost everything. |

| + **Archive File 3** <br>Have at least 150% Trust with Beagle |
| --- |
| At their lowest point, Fang, Kroos, and Beagle [faced expulsion and banishment from the garrison](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/A1_Operations_Preparation_Detachment "A1 Operations Preparation Detachment"). On Kroos's recommendation, the three of them came to Rhodes Island together. On that day, Beagle resolved to repay the kindness of Fang, Kroos, and everyone who stood beside her. |

| + **Archive File 4** <br>Have 200% Trust with Beagle |
| --- |
| After witnessing everyone bicker, play, and express joy day after day, Beagle began to feel grateful and happy. Everyday, Beagle works hard to solve the disputes that arise between [Hibiscus](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Hibiscus "Hibiscus") and [Lava](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lava "Lava"). She helps Fang find Kroos whenever she is slacking off. She even helps the senior members of Rhodes Island by doing chores in her free time. Time and time through and through, she enjoys every moment of her hard work. |
