| Don't be fooled by the way he always lowers his voice to speak, like he's shrouding himself in mystery. He's actually a very good singer.<br>What's with that face? Do you think the Abyssal Hunters are only killing machines? Sure, Ægir's general education doesn't include music or dance classes, but the [Little Handy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Little_Handy "Little Handy") module No.57 has more music than what any terrestrial music critic could hope to have. The domes are like giant pianos, converting the crashing of the waves into sweet melodies. The shifting frequency and movements of the coral by the roads is itself a form of dance... Music and dance are a part of everything we Ægir see and hear. That's how Swordfish, Shark, and I learned how to sing, play instruments, and dance. Our colleagues at the Institutes of Physical Art don't see systematic learning as necessary. They research anything and everything in the ocean and its relation to music and dance to develop a "language" they can use to truly express themselves and communicate with the world.<br>The Captain's even more talented than the rest of us. He's got perfect pitch, and he's a composer too. I heard from Advisor [Blandus](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Blandus "Blandus") that he got an invitation to one of those art institutes when he was young... Right, I mentioned this before: He sees music as nothing more than a tool. Each time we had a hunt in a Seaborn nest, he'd always haphazardly compose a tune, then order everyone in the his team to memorize it. Then after the battle, he'd ask us to sing it back to him and give his opinion. The dregs of the sea can't appreciate art. Memorizing the melody is enough to prove you're still you. So he'd do this before returning to the city for inspection to see who'd be executed and left to dry on the reefs, and who'd be allowed to go home.<br>But there's something else that left a deeper impression on me. Once, during a mission, we ran into two schools of rare fins. They cried out, their chatter all around us, disturbing most of the current. Once we got back to the city, the first thing the Captain did was to upload a score into the port's terminal. I had a listen after that. It was a duet, the cries of the fins poking out between the notes of the main melody. Ever since the Seaborn took over that patch of sea, that duet is the last record of those fins. It's hard to say it was a coincidence, knowing him. It's a beautiful song, but doesn't exactly match his icy expression.<br>...<br>No, I'm not trying to prove anything to you, nor can I prove anything to Ægir. I just wanted to say...<br>He can still sing. Both when [the scum](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Endspeaker "The Endspeaker") devoured [Amaia](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Amaia "Amaia") and the sea around the [Stultifera Navis](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Stultifera_Navis "Stultifera Navis") fell into Silence, and when [Milliarium](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Milliarium "Milliarium")'s beacon tower fell to the seafloor... I could still hear him.<br>—Skadi |
