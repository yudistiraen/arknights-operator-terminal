The mysterious [Durin](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Durin "Durin") girl hailing from the [underground world](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Durin_(country) "Durin (country)") and a close friend of [Myrtle](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Myrtle "Myrtle") is supposed to be a prodigious [Arts](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Arts "Arts") user. However, she is more well-known for her narcolepsy and laziness, which causes her to spend most of her time sleeping. Although Durin's sleepiness does not affect her work performance, it makes others think her people are super lazy until Myrtle's appearance dismisses such a stereotype. It turns out that her narcolepsy is a born defect; what can she do other than keep sleeping all day long...
