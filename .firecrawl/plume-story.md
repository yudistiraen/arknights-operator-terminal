**Leia** the [Lateran](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lateran "Lateran") [Liberi](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Liberi "Liberi") was once a heroic and grateful soldier of the Pontifica Cohors Lateran. However, an [Oripathy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oripathy "Oripathy")-related incident surrounding her coworker Backfire forced her to disobey her seniors, which led to her termination from the Pontifica. Leia has since enlisted herself to [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island") in exchange for Backfire's treatment under the codename "Plume" as a security guard as well as one of the [Doctor](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Doctor "Doctor")'s personal bodyguards.
