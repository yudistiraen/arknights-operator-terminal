Melantha is a young [Feline](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Feline "Feline") from an upper-class family in Victoria, her family's background led to her involvement with spices at a very young age, often assisting her parents in developing new aromatherapy products. She was an intelligent but frail girl with a penchant for arts and literature until she was struck by Oripathy. She came to Rhodes Island for treatment and is now the captain of [Op Reserve A4](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Op_Reserve_A4 "Op Reserve A4"). Although shy and withdrawn, she has a good grasp of how her teammates in A4 behave and how to deal with them, making her an attentive and efficient leader.

## Plot\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Melantha/Story?action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Plot")\]

### Stories of Afternoon\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Melantha/Story?action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Stories of Afternoon")\]

Melantha and [Steward](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Steward "Steward") come across [Vanilla](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Vanilla "Vanilla"), who asks Melantha to help take care of her pets. Since Melantha will be going on the same mission as Vanilla, she is unable to do so, and Steward agrees to help in her stead. Their conversation then shifts to [Franka](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Franka "Franka") teaching swordsmanship to Melantha, with Vanilla expressing slight worry that her senior might corrupt the timid girl. Melantha insists that Franka is very serious when it comes to sword fighting, then apologizes for going overboard with her praises. Shortly after, Melantha excuses herself for a checkup with [Ansel](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ansel "Ansel"), which causes Steward to fret about her condition. Melantha assures him that her infection is under control. As thanks, Vanilla offers to have tea together after her checkup, which makes the Feline visibly happy.[\[1\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Melantha/Story#cite_note-1)

### Adnachiel: Reserve Prophet\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Melantha/Story?action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Adnachiel: Reserve Prophet")\]

Melantha helps handle the provocators by posing as a spice merchant from Londinium. She offers to purchase the main provocator's house, and the lucrative deal distracts him from leading a mob to drive out the Infected patients. She tells him she has little time to waste and wants an answer immediately, then organizes a meeting at a cafe in Council Square.

The team visits [Adnachiel](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Adnachiel "Adnachiel") in prison, where Melantha helps [Cardigan](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Cardigan "Cardigan") pressure him into agreeing to pay for Cardigan's meals for a whole week. Just before they leave, a Lateran messenger arrives, prompting Adnachiel to request temporary leave from Melantha. After Adnachiel explains his reasons for returning to Laterano, Melantha, along with the others sans [Ansel](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ansel "Ansel"), finds his seriousness strange, causing Adnachiel to regret saying so much. He then waves them goodbye and leaves with the Lateran messenger.[\[2\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Melantha/Story#cite_note-2)
