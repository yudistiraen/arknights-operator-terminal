The [Oni](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oni "Oni") codenamed "Noir Corne" has a rather up-and-down life journey. He hails from an infamous clan of [Higashi](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Higashi "Higashi") who has been trying to dissociate himself of the clan's past. But during the Higashi civil war, he was conscripted to be an assassin who was involved in numerous battles such as the "The Bloody Hunt of Nine," the " _Kujin-gari_", and he was quite unpleasant with his job. However, his life changed when he encountered his opponent [Yato](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Yato "Yato") who too shared the same target. Following his surrender over her, there was a rather "romantic" moment – the two decided to desert the army after sparing their target and fled the country forever.

Now a senior combat personnel of [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island"), Noir Corne is currently a member of the [Op A4](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Op_A4 "Op A4") along with [Durin](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Durin "Durin"), [Rangers](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rangers "Rangers") and, most importantly, Yato. He is well-known for wearing his mask all the time without taking off, but to him, he is doing that all for Yato's sake likely as a sign of friendship. As it turns out, Noir is a a very friendly person who can get along with others, especially his juniors, just fine. Perhaps one day, he is even willing to share with you the mystery behind his mask.

### Noir Corne: The Other Side of the Deck\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Noir_Corne/Story?action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Noir Corne: The Other Side of the Deck")\]

Noir Corne plays cards with the other operators and chooses to tap out of the match, blaming his bad luck on his mask choice. He is bewildered when Dylan says his first impression of Noir Corne was that of an old mentor type. Noir Corne discusses the tough battle they just went through and passes on drinking, though his bluff is called out by the others, and he offers to pay for the next round of drinks. When the others tease Chard for his previous breakdown, Noir Corne tells them to stop and listens as Chard admits that he feels ashamed for thinking he could not win the battle. Noir Corne calls it normal and says that his own mindset only changed after coming to [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island"), and cheers Chard up by recalling that [Kal'tsit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Kal%27tsit "Kal'tsit") once commended him. Noir Corne explains that the [Doctor](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Doctor "Doctor") is a normal person like them too. When Dylan recalls a nightmare in which Noir Corne wore an infinite number of masks, the [Oni](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oni "Oni") offers to reveal his face, though the others refuse.

At their urging, he tells a story about an Oni ordered to kill a mother and daughter, only to be stopped by another Oni who was a lackey of a different clan. She was also ordered to kill the girl, but did not want to harm another innocent person, stunning the first Oni. The two, who were in truth Noir Corne and [Yato](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Yato "Yato"), then deserted together. Noir Corne continues that the Oni gained superpowers from wearing masks, and the story ends in a sloppy manner as Noir Corne wins the round. He spots the Doctor in the distance, who greets them, prompting Noir Corne to remark on their good memory and remind them not to wear themselves out. The others excuse themselves, leaving Noir Corne with the Doctor, who offers him a snack. Noir Corne happily accepts and invites the Doctor for a drink.[\[1\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Noir_Corne/Story#cite_note-1)
