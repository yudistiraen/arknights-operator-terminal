The younger twin sister of [Hibiscus](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Hibiscus "Hibiscus"), the [Victorian](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Victoria "Victoria") [Sarkaz](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Sarkaz "Sarkaz") **Lava** grew up in the same middle-class family in [Londinium](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Londinium "Londinium"). While sharing the same sense of compassion as her sister, Lava takes things to her own way as she firmly believes in acting against injustice rather than standing still, a mindset influenced by her time helping her poor and orphaned neighbors back in Londinium.

Lava and Hibiscus joined [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island") together to treat their [Oripathy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oripathy "Oripathy"). In Lava's case she quickly mastered spacial [Originium Arts](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Originium_Arts "Originium Arts") which she utilizes offensively in the battlefield as part of [Op Reserve A1](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Op_Reserve_A1 "Op Reserve A1"). At the same time, she seems to have a _chūnibyō_ behaviour that eventually led her production of a concept B-movie written together with [Nian](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Nian "Nian") where she acted as the protagonist.

The aforementioned movie would eventually inspire Lava's further development upon reaching maturity over the years of her service in [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island"); thanks to the intense training under [Pith](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Pith "Pith")'s guidance and the morale support from Hibiscus, she could now unleash her hidden, outstanding potential in [Originium Arts](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Originium_Arts "Originium Arts"). Adopting the outfit she wore in the movie and utilizing "revolutionary" fire-based Arts, Lava is no longer the same as she were used to be; she is now a formidable caster operative that is on par with R.I.'s other veteran casters.

### Lingering Echoes\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lava/Story?action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Lingering Echoes")\]

Lava makes a small appearance on the R.I. landship. She scolds Hibiscus for her recklessness during the events in Vyseheim, which she had heard from Czerny when she asked him about her former piano teacher. She listens to Hibiscus' attempt at playing her flute, to which she calls as the worst sound she had ever heard in her life.[\[1\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lava/Story#cite_note-1)

### Ancient Forge\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lava/Story?action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Ancient Forge")\]

Lava plays the role of the hero in [Nian](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Nian "Nian")'s B-movie, who is an expert on Catastrophes and the Nian. She disappears from the [Doctor](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Doctor "Doctor") and [Amiya](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Amiya "Amiya")'s sight to check on the marks left by the Nian, concluding that only one of the beasts matches the records. She declares that she will put a stop to them, whoever they are.[\[2\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lava/Story#cite_note-2)

After the [L.G.D.](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lungmen_Guard_Department "Lungmen Guard Department") tracks down the anomalous area without [Originium](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Originium "Originium") reactions, Lava confronts Nian. Lava introduces herself, and grows annoyed when Nian questions her codename. She points out that Nian used the data from Lungmen's records to create the illusions, then presumes that the power she wields is unlike Originium Arts. Lava reveals that she was born to hunt Nian's kind, and senses the change in the air after mentioning Nian's true form. A fight breaks out between the two, and Lava notices how Nian's shield can block her flames. Lava is alarmed when Nian melts down a building to reforge it, so she uses her reserved trick and watches as Nian cowers from the fireworks. Lava explains that old folk sayings unrelated to the Nian were what clued her in to Nian's weakness. She is wary when Nian cuts open the spatial Arts with a sword she crafted, but manages to block her blow with difficulty. Lava asserts that she will fight until she can unravel the mystery.[\[3\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lava/Story#cite_note-3)

The fight ends with Nian summoning her heavenly forge and taking over District 21. Lava is extracted by [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island") at a great price. Three days later, she goes up to Amiya and [Ch'en](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ch%27en "Ch'en") to apologize for engaging Nian. Lava reasons that finding Nian's weakness is the key, and brings news that the beast is eager to communicate despite her terrible personality. She proposes that firecrackers pose no threat if Nian's function is smelting, and reminds them to beware of the sword and shield. Lava states that she will strike Nian's weak point and expresses worry when Amiya says she will come too. Lava then goes to [Snowsant](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Snowsant "Snowsant") to request the blueprint for the underground infrastructure, then gets the idea to use the defense cannons as a two-step firecracker. She thanks [Fumizuki](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Fumizuki "Fumizuki") and is bewildered that Snowsant can draw up the blueprint from memory.

Amiya and Ch'en find Lava again, who states that they will blow the core of the city's cannons after leading Nian there. Lava later ambushes Nian in the city's core district, and prods for information about Nian's siblings. She is shocked that Nian knows of [Hibiscus](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Hibiscus "Hibiscus"). Nian states that she and her eleven siblings have fought against one another for a long time, and Lava supposes they do not get along. The lights go out, and Lava prods for more information by threatening Nian with a suicidal giant firecracker, although she receives nothing of value. Lava then blows the place up. She survives thanks to Nian and is wary when Nian surrenders to the L.G.D., stating that she will interrogate her at Rhodes Island. Nian fades away after bidding farewell to Lava. To Lava's frustration, she realizes that Nian escaped and that it was an illusion that died, becoming confused when Nian returns again. Nian tells Lava that she will not survive the next attack and forges a giant monster from the city.

However, before anything else can happen, the real Lava interrupts Nian's mystical incantation and calls the script nonsensical since Lava was supposed to be the main character and director. She questions the two-step firecracker and the budget for the giant monster, then realizes that Nian somehow grabbed hold of classified operator files. She is then even more puzzled by the sequel-bait scene, reminding Nian that she cannot make a spectacle of [her little sister](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Dusk "Dusk") for a New Year's movie. Lava declares that she will fix her own lines and tells Nian to return the files. When another operator asks about the movie, Lava says to just improvise, then murmurs to herself that she gets the message of the movie and snorts at the lousy villain's death scene.[\[4\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lava/Story#cite_note-4)

### Stories of Afternoon\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lava/Story?action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Stories of Afternoon")\]

Lave helps [Hibiscus](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Hibiscus "Hibiscus") give a recently rescued Perro girl a checkup, bribing her with honey biscuits for an oral exam. It is revealed that the two brought her back to Rhodes Island from the wilderness of Leithanien after subduing her, as she had been attacking passersby while searching for food, including their squad. According to the laws of Leithanien, a rogue Infected like her who had somehow learned Arts was bound to be euthanized. Hibiscus praises Lava for communicating with the Perro and bringing honey biscuits for her, much to her sister's embarrassment.

[Beagle](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Beagle "Beagle") barges into the room and reminds Hibiscus and Lava to attend [Dobermann](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Dobermann "Dobermann")'s class, as she had forgotten to get them out of class. Panicked, they entrust the girl to the first person they see, who happens to be the blacksmith, [Vulcan](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Vulcan "Vulcan"). Some time later, Lava and Hibiscus return from their class and are shocked by [Ceobe](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ceobe "Ceobe")'s development.[\[5\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lava/Story#cite_note-5)

### Lava the Purgatory: All the Same\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lava/Story?action=edit&section=13 "Edit section: Lava the Purgatory: All the Same")\]

Lava and [Hibiscus](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Hibiscus_the_Purifier "Hibiscus the Purifier") visit their parents, who reside in a remote village in Victoria. In her letter addressed to their parents, she expresses suspicion regarding their claim that they are doing fine and that the villagers are tolerant, especially following [the war](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Victorian_Crisis "Victorian Crisis"). Hibiscus intends to prepare dinner, while Lava attempts to sort through their parents' belongings, finding items once belonging to friends, the girls' old workbooks, a pile of photo albums, and stacks of letters. Lava discovers recruitment notices for various jobs, all crossed out, and questions if living in Victoria is worth the struggle, given that their parents were once engineers. Hibiscus admonishes her not to bring it up and insists that they should focus on helping what they can.

Lava goes to borrow chili powder, but the neighbors refuse to hear out her request. As she walks through the village, the local children follow her, roleplaying as Victorian soldiers with Lava as the "devil." Noticing them, Lava intentionally shows off her Arts with fire tricks, reminiscent of what she had done for children in [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island")'s medical bay. She returns to the house to grab materials for a bigger display, which the children mistake for a witchcraft altar. In reality, it is a makeshift projection set for [Nian](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Nian "Nian")'s unreleased movie starring Lava herself. At the climax of the scene, Lava changes into [battle gear](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lava_the_Purgatory/Gallery#Purifying_Flame "Lava the Purgatory/Gallery") and defeats the on-screen [Feranmut](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Feranmut "Feranmut") by burning away the projection sheet. The children are awestruck by the hero in front of them until their guardians approach and drag them back home. Now alone, Lava dismisses them as "noisy little brats," but notices that one of the children left behind a bottle of chili powder. She heads home in a better mood.[\[7\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lava/Story#cite_note-7)
