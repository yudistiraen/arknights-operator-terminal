| **Profile** |
| --- |
| Melantha is the captain of [Reserve Op Team A4](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Op_Reserve_A4 "Op Reserve A4") and a citizen of [Victoria](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Victoria "Victoria"). Before she took up her current post, she was a typical jobless Infected. After an exceptional performance on her tests, she received an offer to join [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island"). |

| **Clinical Analysis** <br>Have at least 25% Trust with Melantha |
| --- |
| Imaging tests have shown the outlines of her internal organs to be indistinct due to abnormal shadows. [Originium](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Originium "Originium") granules have been detected in her circulatory system, confirming her to be infected with [Oripathy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oripathy "Oripathy").<br>**\[Cell-Originium Assimilation\]** 2%<br>Subject is mildly infected. There is a lesion upon the subject's left shoulder, though the infection has largely been contained and is in stable condition.<br>**\[Blood Originium-Crystal Density\]** 0.25u/L<br>Results of internal circulation tests have been positive. Strength training is recommended as part of the course of treatment.<br>'Melantha is still growing and needs to eat food high in nutrients.' - [Medic](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Medic "Medic") Team Intern [Hibiscus](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Hibiscus "Hibiscus") |

| **Archive File 1** <br>Have at least 50% Trust with Melantha |
| --- |
| Melantha was born in [Londinium](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Londinium "Londinium"), the capital of the Kingdom of Victoria. Her father is a trader engaged in the bulk spice trade. He runs several spice mills of his own. Her mother is the head of the family's aromatherapy flagship store in the heart of Londinium.<br>Following in the footsteps of her parents, Melantha got involved in spices at a very young age, often assisting them in developing new aromatherapy productions. Melantha's experiences have been heavily influenced by her parents. |

| **Archive File 2** <br>Have at least 100% Trust with Melantha |
| --- |
| Melantha was a student of the Wessex Private Middle School when she was young. She received exceptional marks in all her classes, but because she was soft spoken and lacked communication skills, she was never the center of attention or very popular.<br>Her lack of friends remains due to her unwillingness to change her personality. With her parents' help, she immersed herself into upper-class academic activities, including art, literature, and poetry. In traditional Victorian society, the arts are an important part of a woman's formal education.<br>In addition, in order to strengthen her weak constitution, Melantha's parents arranged for her to study swordsmanship and other popular upper-class sports.<br>After the [Infected](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Infected "Infected") crisis broke out, Melantha was herself Infected. Her parents arranged for her to receive treatment from Rhodes Island Pharmaceutical's.\[sic\] And before long, she was on the way to the company for treatment. |

| **Archive File 3** <br>Have at least 150% Trust with Melantha |
| --- |
| Melantha has a close relationship with all the other members of Op Reserve Team A4. Introverted by nature, she initially worried about whether she would be able to serve well in her role as Op Reserve Team A4's captain, but with the help of her companions on the team, she broke through her inner obstacles.<br>The lively and cheerful [Cardigan](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Cardigan "Cardigan") is her inseparable friend. In both life and on the battlefield, they maintain a strong and complementary relationship.<br>Melantha also has great respect for the mature and dependable [Operator](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Operator "Operator") [Steward](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Steward "Steward"). She feels both guilty and grateful to [Ansel](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ansel "Ansel"), who is in charge of maintaining her health records as well as providing her with daily treatment. Finally, [Adnachiel](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Adnachiel "Adnachiel") is the Operator that Melantha understands the least. Since the beginning, she has struggled to figure out what this kid from [Laterano](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Laterano "Laterano") might be thinking. Nevertheless, Adnachiel hasn't caused any problems in battle.<br>During her tenure as captain, her subordinates have all spoken highly of her attentive and careful nature. Her serious and disciplined qualities have enabled her to complete all the missions Rhodes Island has entrusted to her. |

| **Archive File 4** <br>Have 200% Trust with Melantha |
| --- |
| So far, both existing archives and the observations of her fellow Operators have failed to grasp just what kind of swordsmanship Melantha employs. This is one of Melantha's few mysteries.<br>Judging from test records and daily battle videos, her swordsmanship appears to have originated from the Victorian traditional duelist's style, but at some point during her training, she must have picked up some heavy influences from Eastern sword arts.<br>Melantha's weapon can also be looked at as a kind of circumstantial evidence for this. She uses an exceptionally fine yet extremely sharp straight sword. Current data makes clear that this weapon is only used in Yan and some parts of the Far East.<br>Melantha's swordsmanship is agile and her skills are outstanding. In terms of combat techniques, Melantha has is skilled in tactical assault.\[sic\] Even without support, she can rely upon her mobility to move about the battlefield and hide when necessary.<br>On the other hand, Melantha lacks the ability for effective direct confrontation and she displays certain deficiencies in stability during high-intensity operations.<br>Up until now, Melantha has trained under the guidance of [Franka](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Franka "Franka"). |
