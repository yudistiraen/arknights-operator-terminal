| − **Profile** |
| --- |
| Vanilla is a [Blacksteel](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Blacksteel "Blacksteel") trainee, currently training and studying as part of exchange program with [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island").<br>She has learned to use her race's combat abilities to her advantage, and performs well as a [Vanguard](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Vanguard "Vanguard"). |

| + **Clinical Analysis** <br>Have at least 25% Trust with Vanilla |
| --- |
| Imaging test for this [Operator](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Operator "Operator") showed clear outline of internal organs without any unusual dark spot. No unusual traces of [Originium](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Originium "Originium") particles present in her system, no signs of infection, confirming her as Non-infected.<br>**\[Cell-Originium Assimilation\]** 0%<br>Operator Vanilla showed no symptoms of [Oripathy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oripathy "Oripathy").<br>**\[Blood Originium-Crystal Density\]** 0.12u/L<br>Operator Vanilla rarely comes into contact with Originium. |

| + **Archive File 1** <br>Have at least 50% Trust with Vanilla |
| --- |
| Vanilla is a rather naive and inexperienced Operator sent to us from Blacksteel Worldwide.<br>Even though she's only been with Blacksteel for a short time, her dedication impressed the executives enough for them to send her to Rhodes Island for additional training.<br>She stands much taller than [Liskarm](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Liskarm "Liskarm") or [Franka](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Franka "Franka"), but the three are often seen together. |

| + **Archive File 2** <br>Have at least 100% Trust with Vanilla |
| --- |
| Vanilla is very protective of animals. A conversation about animals will always gets her out of her normally very thick shell. It may also be a reason she left home.<br>But because the animals she strives to protect include icky things like the [Originium snail](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Infected_Creature#Originium_Slugs "Infected Creature"), her affection can unnerve some people. |

| + **Archive File 3** <br>Have at least 150% Trust with Vanilla |
| --- |
| Because Vanilla was born in an [isolated tribe](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Sargon#Vouivre_Alliance "Sargon"), Blacksteel added a special common knowledge to her training regimen.<br>According to Franka, Vanilla was at first startled by the automatic doors at the base.<br>In fact, Vanilla is not yet used to most of the technological equipment at Rhodes Island. |
