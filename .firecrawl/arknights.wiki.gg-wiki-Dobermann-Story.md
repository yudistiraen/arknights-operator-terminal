The resident [Perro](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Perro "Perro") drill instructor of [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island"), **Dobermann** was a colonel of the [Bolívarian](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Bol%C3%ADvar "Bolívar") Coalition Government who later defected to the True Bolívarians. However, she realized neither side intended to make her homeland a better place and that they were no different from the Singas dynasty. As a result of her realization and upon contracting [Oripathy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oripathy "Oripathy"), she joined the politically neutral R.I. for treatment. In exchange, Dobermann became the instructor everyone in R.I. knows and respects, primarily training new recruits through a strict regimen emphasizing discipline. On the battlefield, Dobermann can also act and join the battle herself when needed, using her whip to lash enemies from a short distance away.

During the briefing about [Viscount Walden](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Walden_Caster "Walden Caster")'s request for [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island") to rescue his daughter, Dobermann agrees with [Kal'tsit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Kal%27tsit "Kal'tsit")'s caution, noting that Viscount Walden is known for having lost his son to the Infected a few years ago and his strict policies toward them. Dobermann adds that Walden's surname is Caster, and that the family of the [Duke of Caster](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Duke_of_Caster "Duke of Caster") is highly influential in Western Victoria. She points out that forming connections with them will be very helpful for Rhodes Island's operations in [Victoria](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Victoria "Victoria").[\[1\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Dobermann/Story#cite_note-1)

### Operational Intelligence\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Dobermann/Story?action=edit&section=9 "Edit section: Operational Intelligence")\]

Dobermann gives a briefing to [Flamebringer](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Flamebringer "Flamebringer"), who has just joined Rhodes Island. Aware of his recent skirmish with [Reunion](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Reunion "Reunion"), she reminds him that Rhodes Island rejects those who violate regulations and tells him not to do such a thing again. She then welcomes him aboard and escorts him to Medical.[\[2\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Dobermann/Story#cite_note-2)

### Stories of Afternoon\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Dobermann/Story?action=edit&section=10 "Edit section: Stories of Afternoon")\]

Dobermann is in the midst of cleaning her room when [Fang](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Fang "Fang") and [Beagle](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Beagle "Beagle") arrive to give her their team's test results. Dobermann receives the results and, despite her reassuring words, she tells Beagle to inform Kroos about a session that afternoon. Curious about Dobermann's past, Fang offers to help her clean her room. The offer surprises Dobermann, who had always thought of them as fearful of her. As they help her sort through the multitude of items in her room, Dobermann answers their questions and explains to them the three factions in [Bolívar](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Bol%C3%ADvar "Bolívar"): the puppet Singas dynasty of Leithanien, the Columbian-sponsored Coalition Government, and the grassroots resistance force, the True Bolívarians. She explains that she was once a colonel serving the autonomous government. Beagle sneezes while wiping dust and comes across metal tags, which Dobermann explains belonged to soldiers in the army. A flashback reveals that they were comrades she wasn't close with, but whose deaths caused her to become disillusioned.

She then asks the two trainees what they think is the defining characteristic of a soldier. She dismisses their initial answer and explains that numbers are only one part of it. Beagle answers correctly, and Dobermann points out that it is obedience to the chain of command, which is why the correct orders must be issued. She reveals that she defected to the resistance but eventually left, and was pleasantly surprised when she moved to Rhodes Island. Although the two trainees fail to understand her philosophies, Dobermann tells them to appreciate Rhodes Island. The two then hurriedly leave when they realize it is already lunchtime. Left alone, Dobermann reflects on her teaching at Rhodes Island, willing to believe in the future [Amiya](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Amiya "Amiya") and [Kal'tsit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Kal%27tsit "Kal'tsit") envisioned, but resolves to one day return to Bolívar.[\[3\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Dobermann/Story#cite_note-3)

### Children of Ursus\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Dobermann/Story?action=edit&section=11 "Edit section: Children of Ursus")\]

Dobermann supervises [Leto](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Leto "Leto") and [Natalya](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rosa "Rosa")'s training as Natalya practices with her modified siege weapon. Dobermann tells Natalya that she lacks stamina and is still far from combat-readiness, although her being [Ursus](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ursus "Ursus") gives her a nice base. Dobermann commends the girl's willpower. Dobermann and Grace instruct them for the training session. Dobermann also greets the [Doctor](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Doctor "Doctor"), but tells them they should be resting during lunchtime. She surmises that [Amiya](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Amiya "Amiya") must have sent them, because back when the four girls applied, Amiya was against the idea but changed her mind at some point. Although she understands Amiya's plight, Dobermann says they can only teach them how to fight and protect themselves.[\[4\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Dobermann/Story#cite_note-4)

### Vulcan: Forging\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Dobermann/Story?action=edit&section=19 "Edit section: Vulcan: Forging")\]

Dobermann allows [Vulcan](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Vulcan "Vulcan") to use the training facilities to test the shield she had repaired, even offering to have one or two trainees help her with the testing. When one of the reserve operators hesitates at Vulcan's request for them to not hold back, Dobermann instructs him to follow the request, explaining that Vulcan is stronger than he is and that she had forged the shield using a new method. After the reserve operator tires out, Vulcan asks Dobermann to try the shield out herself. Dobermann lashes at Vulcan, and compliments the shield.[\[6\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Dobermann/Story#cite_note-6)
