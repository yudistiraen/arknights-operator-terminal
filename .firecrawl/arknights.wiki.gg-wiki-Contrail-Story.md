The sole daughter of a pair of Columbian biologists, **Claudia** spent her childhood following her parents' travel across Terra. After discovering her love of flying, she dreams of developing a flying device that is capable of taking her soaring high up in the sky.[\[1\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Contrail/Story#cite_note-1)

She is currently taking [Oripathy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oripathy "Oripathy") treatment in [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island_(landship) "Rhodes Island (landship)") after crashing into a [Catastrophe](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Catastrophe "Catastrophe") cloud during one of her test flights.[\[2\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Contrail/Story#cite_note-oprec-2)

## Story\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Contrail/Story?action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Story")\]

### Contrail: Eve of Migration\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Contrail/Story?action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Contrail: Eve of Migration")\]

After a failed test flight, Claudia stays inside her house to recuperate. After four days of rest, her mother asks about her condition, to which Claudia answers that her leg is still swollen and presses on what her parents are hiding from her. She deduces she had crashed into a [Catastrophe](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Catastrophe "Catastrophe") cloud, and her mother reveals Claudia now has Oripathy. Afterwards, Claudia works herself to the bone in the workshop, postponing her promise to eat and talk together with her parents. Her mother invites her to cook dinner with her, and Claudia apologizes for locking herself up, but confesses she is afraid of dying when she has not realized her dream of flying alongside a fowlbeast migration. Her mother then receives a message from Claudia's father, who has found a nest of fowlbeast eggs.

Claudia helps her mother make a nest for the eggs. The fowlbeasts imprint on Claudia after hatching, and she brings them on a walk in the fields to get sunlight. Claudia's mother recalls that Claudia also had two little fowlbeasts they found as eggs in Rim Billiton. She then cautiously asks Claudia about the fowlbeasts' life cycle, saying that it is fine if they stay with them and do not migrate, prompting Claudia to question if she means to say it is fine if they do not fly. Her mother then admits that she fears burdening Claudia with her words of encouragement and does not want her to suffer any more. Looking at the little fowlbeast, Claudia says she might end up suffering, but will never grow afraid of flying.

With her mother's support, she continues working on her flyer, just in time to accompany the fowlbeasts' first flight, although she laments that she cannot follow them for the full migration. Her doubts dissipate in the sky, and Claudia resolves to fly anywhere she can, since her dream is simply to fly. Afterwards, Claudia tells her mother that she is thinking of applying to [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island") for treatment as well as to research the migratory patterns of fowlbeasts. Her mother approves and plans to check the visitation rules, and they share a hug.[\[2\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Contrail/Story#cite_note-oprec-2)
