| Initially a patient at [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island"), Popukar requested and passed the trial to become an [Operator](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Operator "Operator") for Rhodes Island. She was assigned to [Op Reserve Team A6](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Op_Reserve_A6 "Op Reserve A6").<br>She possesses tremendous destructive power, becoming the team's heavy. |

| + **Clinical Analysis** <br>Have at least 25% Trust with Popukar |
| --- |
| Imaging test for this Operator showed blurry outline of internal organs with visible unusual dark spots. Unusually high concentration of [Originium](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Originium "Originium") particles were present in her circulation, indicating signs of infection and confirming her as infected at this stage.<br>**\[Cell-Originium Assimilation\]** 3%<br>Crystallization visible on surface of body, with many symptoms of [Oripathy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oripathy "Oripathy").<br>**\[Blood Originium-Crystal Density\]** 0.2u/L<br>Subject is currently stabilized, but regular observation and treatment are required. |

| + **Archive File 1** <br>Have at least 50% Trust with Popukar |
| --- |
| A member of Op Reserve Team A6. Although not capable of complex reasoning, her infection bestowed on her an ability that made her a suitable Operator.<br>The source of a lot of problems for Team A6, most of these are unintentional. Because of her sincerity, she is also the easiest to forgive. |

| + **Archive File 2** <br>Have at least 100% Trust with Popukar |
| --- |
| Unlike most other members, Popukar was taken in by Dr. [Kal'tsit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Kal%27tsit "Kal'tsit") personally.<br>Dr. Kal'tsit did not explain why she made this choice, only saying Popukar would stay on as a full-time member of Rhodes Island.<br>However, Dr. [Ansel](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ansel "Ansel") said that was the first time he had ever seen Dr. Kal'tsit visibly upset. |

| + **Archive File 3** <br>Have at least 150% Trust with Popukar |
| --- |
| Although Popukar failed her Operator exam, she was allowed to work in logistics, rather than being cast out.<br>And as it is so rare for an infection to make a patient stronger, even rarer to the extent of Popukar's infection, she is a very valuable case study. |

| + **Archive File 4** <br>Have 200% Trust with Popukar |
| --- |
| Popukar has gradually become one of the mascots of Rhodes Island. After all, no one could hate such an energetic little cutie — even if she occasionally engages in "minor" vandalism.<br>Moreover, since joining A6, she has a much bigger family. Their shenanigans are just part of the Rhodes Island color nowadays. |
