Born in the southern territory of [Kjerag](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Kjerag "Kjerag") to a family with the same surname as his codename, Steward's talent for [Originium Arts](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Originium_Arts "Originium Arts") was left buried in the snow. When the Silverash clan declared a new economic policy that brought merchants from various countries to Kjerag, Steward freelanced as a guide and local servicer, as well as a model to help market goods. It is mentioned that he learned Arts from a psychic whose casting method is similar to [Lava](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lava "Lava")'s. At one point, he befriended [Adnachiel](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Adnachiel "Adnachiel") and [Cardigan](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Cardigan "Cardigan"), but contracted Oripathy along with the former while traveling in [Leithanien](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Leithanien "Leithanien"). Cardigan's desperate search for medical aid led them to receive help from a [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island") Operator. After an exceptional performance on his tests, he joined Rhodes Island and now serves on a reserve team, [Op Reserve A4](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Op_Reserve_A4 "Op Reserve A4").

## Plot\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Steward/Story?action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Plot")\]

### Stories of Afternoon\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Steward/Story?action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Stories of Afternoon")\]

[Melantha](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Melantha "Melantha") and Steward come across [Vanilla](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Vanilla "Vanilla"), who asks for Melantha's help in taking care of her pets. Steward agrees to help in Melantha's stead. Their conversation then shifts to [Franka](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Franka "Franka") teaching swordsmanship to Melantha. Steward grows concerned for Melantha's condition when she excuses herself for a checkup with [Ansel](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ansel "Ansel"), but she reassures him that her infection is under control.

Steward and Vanilla talk about how Melantha's mood has improved recently, and then about the cooperation between [Blacksteel](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Blacksteel "Blacksteel") and Rhodes Island. [Blaze](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Blaze "Blaze") joins the conversation, appearing with Dora, a lively little girl who was recently admitted to the ship for treatment. As Dora leaves for class, Steward yells at her not to run in the hallways and winces when she trips and falls. He then recalls that in his team, [Cardigan](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Cardigan "Cardigan") enjoys playing with the children in the treatment ward, while Ansel often stays late to tell them stories. A call from [Amiya](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Amiya "Amiya") soon pulls Blaze away.

Steward accompanies Vanilla to her room and is stunned by her pets and their unique personalities. After she reminisces about her hometown in [Sargon](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Sargon "Sargon"), Steward tells her about his hometown in Kjerag and adds that her request had made Melantha happy.[\[1\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Steward/Story#cite_note-1)

### Istina: Let's Go, Let's Go\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Steward/Story?action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Istina: Let's Go, Let's Go")\]

On the day of the food festival, Steward and the other operators visit the booth run by the [U.S.S.G.](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ursus_Student_Self-Governing_Group "Ursus Student Self-Governing Group") girls. [Cardigan](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Cardigan "Cardigan") is amazed at the number of booths and the variety of food and is worried that she cannot eat it all. Steward tells her to calm down, as the festival runs all day.[\[2\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Steward/Story#cite_note-2)

### Adnachiel: Reserve Prophet\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Steward/Story?action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Adnachiel: Reserve Prophet")\]

To help solve the problem in Ashford, Steward hides his infection and influences public opinion to accept the [Infected](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Infected "Infected") as the labor that will help develop the plate for them. He spends time mingling with the locals so that public opinion won't force the nobles' hand. The team visits [Adnachiel](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Adnachiel "Adnachiel") in prison, and Steward comments he looks nothing like a despairing prisoner. When Adnachiel prepares to leave, Steward asks if he can expect to see him with a gun. In response, Adnachiel explains his reason for returning to Laterano, mentioning that he is not going back for the right to carry a gun. Steward, along with the others sans Ansel, finds Adnachiel's seriousness to be strange, causing the Sankta to regret saying so much.[\[3\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Steward/Story#cite_note-3)
