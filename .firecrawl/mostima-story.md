A [Lateran](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Laterano "Laterano") _Legatus_ ( _Nuntia Apostolici_) currently under “sanctioned” employment with [Penguin Logistics](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Penguin_Logistics "Penguin Logistics"). A fallen [Sankta](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Sankta "Sankta") with a dimmed halo and wings, as well as [Sarkaz](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Sarkaz "Sarkaz") traits: black horns and a tail. Mostima maintains a cheerful demeanor but keeps her distance from both friends and strangers, making her difficult to contact or approach. When Penguin Logistics partnered with [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island"), Mostima lent her support, wielding a pair of ancient [Kazdelian](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Kazdel "Kazdel") staves known as the “Black Lock” and “White Key” to channel temporal [Arts](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Arts "Arts") that slow the perception of time. This power carries its own risks, as the Lock and Key house the preserved consciousness of a long-dead [Feranmut](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Feranmut "Feranmut") called the ["Lifebone"](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lifebone "Lifebone"), capable of spatial and limited temporal manipulation. Currently, only Mostima can control the Lifebone’s relic.

Mostima is [Lemuen](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lemuen "Lemuen")'s sister-in-arms and was close with Lemuen's sister [Lemuel](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Exusiai "Exusiai") since childhood. An altercation in [Kazdel](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Kazdel "Kazdel") forced her to shoot [Andoain](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Andoain "Andoain"), leading to her fall. Despite her status as an outcast, Mostima continues to watch over Lemuen and Exusiai from a distance.

### Mostima: Journey's Footnote\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Mostima/Story?action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Mostima: Journey's Footnote")\]

Mostima returns to a town called Deeproot, whose locals her team once assisted after it was destroyed by a [Catastrophe](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Catastrophe "Catastrophe"). They had escorted the non-Sankta residents to the town's current location, brought the [Sanktas](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Sankta "Sankta") back to Laterano, and even took out a year's wages in advance to request supplies for the townsfolk. There, she witnesses a dispute arise over the construction of a new statue between Melissa, a Sankta from Deeproot who returned from Laterano and wishes to use her skills to contribute to the town, and Susan, the daughter of the original statue's sculptor, who wants to recreate it along with its unique scent. The two compete over the right to construct the statue. Mostima greets the surprised townsfolk and offers to bring back Susan, replying that her team is still doing well when asked by the mayor. She promises Melissa that they can talk later, slipping away just before [Lemuel](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Exusiai "Exusiai") can see her.

Mostima recalls the time when [Andoain](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Andoain "Andoain") explained the reason behind his frustration, back when all they could do for the non-Sankta refugees was take them to new land and send supplies. She muses that Melissa could satisfy Lemuel for a while by sharing knowledge of her past, then goes to find Susan near the hills. The [Kuranta](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Kuranta "Kuranta") girl explains that her father often came to this spot to see the entire town, though now all she can see is a broken ruin. Mostima then excuses herself to inspect the ruins and discovers the original statue, remarking to her staves that the secret behind the statue's scent is the mingled scent of soil and stone. It is not exactly a pleasant scent, but certainly a unique one.

She then finds Lemuel and Melissa detonating a large rock meant for sculpting, grabs Lemuel to stop her from falling into a hole, and warns her to be careful. She disappears by the time the dust settles. Later, Mostima and Susan present the scented soil to the mayor and Melissa. Melissa thanks them but remarks that there's no reason she can't use the scent as well, to which Mostima replies that it's a cunning move. The mayor thanks Mostima, and Melissa asks why she came to help them today. Mostima recalls the time she had fallen, and afterward, when she broke into the Curia to discover what Andoain had seen while holding the Lock and Key, as it had not reacted to her when she retrieved it before. She then saw a fantastical plane and understood why Andoain had gone mad. Unlike [Fiammetta](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Fiammetta "Fiammetta"), who was enraged by Andoain's actions, Mostima had always known that she and Andoain would walk separate paths. He chose to pursue the answers to his questions, while she chose to turn back.

Mostima tells them that she once thought things were terrible, but did not struggle with what she saw, then lowers her hood to reveal her Fallen state. After traveling the lands as a _Legatus_, she concludes that the Sanktas are lovely bastards, and tells Melissa to inform Lemuel that she'll be in [Lungmen](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lungmen "Lungmen") to meet a someone called [Emperor](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Emperor "Emperor"). Walking away, she reflects that she understands Andoain better now and laments that they should have shared a few more drinks together. Mostima thinks that she will not make the same choices, nor will she welcome him warmly, but concludes that all they can do is focus on what lies before them, just as she has been doing while taking Lemuel around [Terra](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Terra "Terra").[\[1\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Mostima/Story#cite_note-1)

### A Kazdelian Rescue\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Mostima/Story?action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: A Kazdelian Rescue")\]

[Nymph](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Nymph "Nymph") encounters Mostima, who is meeting [Crownie Mantel](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Crownie_Mantel "Crownie Mantel") beyond the city. Mostima surprises Nymph with her presence, assuring her she's only there to deliver supplies. She explains her connection to Crownie, revealing that Crownie once worked in [Ursus](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ursus "Ursus") but returned to Kazdel. Mostima also shares information about increased caravan traffic to Kazdel and Rhodes Island's established connections, noting that Crownie will no longer require her logistical services. Her bodyguard, [Fiammetta](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Fiammetta "Fiammetta"), is confused by how far she went for this delivery.[\[2\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Mostima/Story#cite_note-2)
