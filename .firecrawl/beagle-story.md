The young [Bolívarian](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Bol%C3%ADvar "Bolívar") [Perro](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Perro "Perro") Beagle is a former [Columbian](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Columbia "Columbia") police cadet who worked in Tkaronto with [Fang](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Fang "Fang") and [Kroos](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Kroos "Kroos"). Following a [Catastrophe](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Catastrophe "Catastrophe") codenamed "The Blackwasp" that struck the city, Beagle contracted severe [Oripathy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oripathy "Oripathy"), and she went with her colleagues to [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island") for treatment. After becoming an R.I. operator to avoid discrimination as [Infected](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Infected "Infected"), they formed the [Op Reserve Team A1](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Op_Reserve_Team_A1 "Op Reserve Team A1"), where Beagle puts her police training to good use as a defender on the battlefield. Beagle is somewhat childish and clumsy, but she demonstrates that she is a capable combatant.

At some point, Beagle faced a brutal challenge in Bolívar as her "final lesson."[\[2\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Beagle/Story#cite_note-2) Although it is suspected that she is injured or (worse) died,[\[3\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Beagle/Story#cite_note-3) it is revealed in [Fang the Fire-Sharpened](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Fang_the_Fire-Sharpened "Fang the Fire-Sharpened")'s Archive Files that Beagle is alive and well, and has since retired from combat duty to work as an engineer in R.I.'s Engineering Department, thus the former is more likely to be the case.

### Stories of Afternoon\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Beagle/Story?action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Stories of Afternoon")\]

Reserve Team A1 has just finished their tests, with Beagle failing hers. She and [Kroos](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Kroos "Kroos") gather to see [Fang](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Fang "Fang"). Instructor Grace assures them that the tests were not designed to be passed easily, as there is a large skill gap between trainees and instructors. He congratulates them for their efforts against the other instructors, who are experts in their own right, having been trained by a much stricter [Dobermann](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Dobermann "Dobermann"). The three Operators listen as Grace reminisces that Dobermann had been like a real soldier during the early days of Rhodes Island, and that she had always cared about people. Fang offers to take the test results to Dobermann for Grace, and Beagle accompanies her.

Beagle can only laugh nervously when Dobermann tells her to inform Kroos about a session that afternoon. As they help her sort through the multitude of items in her room, the two ask questions about her past. Dobermann explains to them the three factions in Bolívar and tells them she was once a colonel serving the autonomous government. Beagle sneezes while wiping away dust and comes across metal tags, which Dobermann explains had belonged to soldiers in the army. Having Bolívarian parents, Beagle manages to read the names on the tags.

When Dobermann asks the two what they think is the defining characteristic of a soldier, Beagle first answers weaponry but eventually comes close to the correct answer. Dobermann points out that it is obedience to the chain of command, which is why the correct orders must be issued. Although the two trainees fail to fully grasp her philosophies, Dobermann tells them to appreciate Rhodes Island. The two then hurriedly leave when they realize it is already lunchtime.[\[4\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Beagle/Story#cite_note-4)

Beagle barges into the room and reminds [Hibiscus](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Hibiscus "Hibiscus") and [Lava](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lava "Lava"), who are giving [Ceobe](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ceobe "Ceobe") a checkup, to attend Dobermann's class. When pressed about her earlier words, she stammers and admits she might have forgotten to excuse them from class, sending the two into a panic.[\[5\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Beagle/Story#cite_note-5)

### Kroos: Apples\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Beagle/Story?action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Kroos: Apples")\]

Kroos has slipped away once again, so Beagle tells Fang not to be mad at her. When Fang asks what she usually does, Beagle explains that she just watches Kroos work like normal. Fang then spots Kroos and tells Beagle to chase after her. Beagle ends up mistaking [Leonhardt](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Leonhardt "Leonhardt") for Kroos and tells Fang to go to the cafeteria. There, she apologizes to Fang for her mistake. Seeing Fang determined to track Kroos down, Beagle notes that she looks really mad this time. When Fang tells her that Kroos said she would bring back a present since today is Fang's anniversary as captain, Beagle remarks that she will scold Kroos herself, then wishes Fang a happy anniversary.

The two then spot Kroos in a hurry and follow her to [Mayer](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Mayer "Mayer")'s workshop. Beagle expresses surprise that Kroos is actually busy. She follows Fang back to their room so that they can clean up Kroos's mess and get something to eat. Later that night, Beagle welcomes Kroos back, the [Cautus](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Cautus "Cautus") having brought muffins for Fang, and says that they are going to bed. Beagle notes that Kroos is too shy to actually ask Fang for permission to slack off, but she is a thoughtful person. Beagle explains that Kroos is close to Fang, as she does not treat others the same way, but the Kuranta sighs and wishes Kroos would treat her normally. In reply, Beagle tells her to relax and adds that she will drag Kroos to tomorrow's training if needed, then answers Fang with a good night.[\[6\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Beagle/Story#cite_note-6)
