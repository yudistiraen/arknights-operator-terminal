A list of **1-star** ( **1★**) [Operators](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Operator "Operator"). All but two of them are robots under the service of [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island") and their allies/associates, while the other two are non-combatants/civilians who are only counted on in emergency situations.

1★ Operators can be distinguished by the following:

- They have a maximum level of 30.
- They have one [talent](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Talent "Talent") which is upgraded as their [Potential](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Potential "Potential") improves. The talent always activates when the Operator is deployed.
- They are not counted towards the Unit Limit but have a redeployment time of 200 seconds; because of this, their traits are always shown as the following:

| --- | --- | --- |
| “ | _{branch trait} and ignores the Deployment Limit, but has a long Redeployment Time._ | ” |
