A list of **2-star** ( **2★**) [Operators](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Operator "Operator"). While representing experienced combatants of the [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island")'s paramilitary force, they have certain flaws that prevent them from keeping up with the others (e.g. [Durin](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Durin "Durin")'s narcolepsy, [Rangers](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rangers "Rangers")' old age, [Yato](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Yato "Yato")'s photosensitivity).

2★ Operators can be distinguished by the following:

- They have a maximum level of 30.
- They have one [talent](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Talent "Talent") which is available at Level 30.

All 2★ Operators notably share the "Starter" [recruitment](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Recruitment "Recruitment") tag.

**Note**: _All 2★ Operators have been released in the Global server as of January 2024, and their Global release status is omitted._
