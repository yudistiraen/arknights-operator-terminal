**Hildegard** (not to be confused with Hildegard Grimmacht, one of the [Twin Empresses](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Twin_Empresses "Twin Empresses") of [Leithanien](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Leithanien "Leithanien")), a [Liberi](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Liberi "Liberi") of partial [Victorian](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Victoria "Victoria") descent, is a nun from the Landen Monastery in [Laterano](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Laterano "Laterano") and a member of the Landen Guards. She has traveled across [Terra](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Terra "Terra") to sell the Monastery's dairy and wine products to prevent its imminent closure due to monetary concerns. Because of this, she often gets mistaken for a traveling businesswoman rather than a devoted nun.

Eventually, Hildegard was brought into the service of [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island") under the recommendation of [Emperor](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Emperor "Emperor") and [Eurill Pides](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Eurill_Pides "Eurill Pides"). There, she operates under the codename "Archetto," making good use of her archery skills courtesy of her mandatory training and experience as the Guard to provide ranged combat support on the battlefield.

### Beyond Here\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Archetto/Story?action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Beyond Here")\]

To solve the financial problems of Landen Monastery, Hildegard leaves for [Lungmen](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lungmen "Lungmen"). She dreams of the monastery becoming a wine brewery before meeting [Bison](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Bison "Bison"). Hildegard insists on rejecting the reimbursement and explains that she will be taking a look around Lungmen. She then inquires how Bison can treat his father merely as his boss while working, her boldness surprising him. The two part on good terms, Hildegard seeing Bison's ambition to surpass his father and Bison finding her moderation admirable, and Hildegard agrees to share her stories of [Laterano](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Laterano "Laterano") with Bison once she has spare time.

Hildegard lines up at a crowded dessert shop and leaves with overly sweet cakes after some time, coming across [Snowsant](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Snowsant "Snowsant"), who recognizes her as the Lateran visitor staying in the room across from Snowsant's temporary one. They introduce themselves, and Hildegard offers cakes to her, which the shy [Liberi](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Liberi "Liberi") accepts. They spend the day together, discussing the lands they have visited. Snowsant describes [Columbia](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Columbia "Columbia"), while Hildegard explains about Landen Monastery. It was a convent for training Apostolic warriors that accepted people from other countries, but it has lost all work to the [Notarial Hall](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Notarial_Hall "Notarial Hall") and received no grants from the Pope on the grounds that the monastery must learn to become self-reliant, leaving them teetering on the edge of bankruptcy. Hildegard coughs from her outburst, then adds that they still have their wheat fields. They reach the residence and part ways.

On the day of the business meeting, Hildegard finally gets the chance to meet [Eurill Pides](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Eurill_Pides "Eurill Pides") at a bar. She is impressed by his business strategies, and when she asks about him choosing Landen Monastery, he explains about the vision he has for the project, stunning Hildegard. She asks whether Bison knows about it, to which Eurill replies that he does not. She admits that her own goal seems rather simplistic in comparison, and is reassured by Eurill that cherishing one's homeland is not a self-serving pursuit.

After the meeting, Hildegard returns to her room, amazed yet unsettled by Eurill's words. She then hears an explosion and sees [Penguin Logistics](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Penguin_Logistics "Penguin Logistics") chasing a thief through the streets. Shortly after, Hildegard lets Snowsant stay in her room and the Liberi explains who the Penguin Logistics girls are. The mention of [Sora](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Sora "Sora") being an idol piques Hildegard's interest, prompting Snowsant to turn on the television so they can watch a music concert together. The next morning, Hildegard wakes to find Snowsant asleep and decides to take a walk around Lungmen, pondering the choices she can make to travel across [Terra](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Terra "Terra"). Along the way, she encounters [Emperor](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Emperor "Emperor"), who encourages her to see the world and think bigger, remarking that she is easy on the eyes, just like Paul Landen, the monastery's founder who laid down his gun to take up a pen. Before Hildegard can respond, Emperor gets into a car and leaves.

Before Hildegard's departure from Lungmen, she asks Eurill why he is always at the bar. He tells her that he happens to like the ambiance, as well as the name "The Ends of the Earth." They toast to the project, and Hildegard muses that she wishes to see more of the world. She cites a fable called "La Bestia Superbia," in which an arrogant man slandered a Lateran mural because he believed it depicted a vile beast. She remarks that Eurill's goal of shattering arrogance between nations will be an arduous task and expresses her admiration. Relieved, Eurill asks her to one day take his son to Laterano so he can study further, as his mind has broadened somewhat after meeting [Mostima](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Mostima "Mostima"). Hildegard finds the name familiar, noting that Sankta can live good lives no matter their situation due to their easygoing nature. She then receives a contact address for Rhodes Island and is told that Emperor wanted her to have it.[\[2\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Archetto/Story#cite_note-2)

### Archetto: Idol Promotion\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Archetto/Story?action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Archetto: Idol Promotion")\]

One day, Archetto was walking on a street in [Lungmen](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lungmen "Lungmen"), when she was scouted by an idol manager. Interested by the prospect of the opportunity to advertise products with the popularity of an idol, she signed a contract with the manager.

Sometime after, the day of Archetto's meet-and-greet and album-signing session has arrived. However, her booth was completely devoid of customers since early morning. After Archetto accidentally napped for half an hour, in which, she dreamt about Landen Monastery's closing and [Exusiai](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Exusiai "Exusiai")'s explosive demolition countdown, she and the manager conversed about her lack of popularity. The manager determined that the main reason Archetto didn't have a single fan was that she hadn't delivered the image of a nun to the audience, to which Archetto remarked that she has a qualm about always wearing a persona during the job.

The manager left to find some way to attract customers to her booth, and during her absence, an old lady came to her booth and inquired her about the free bread giveaway that was written on the displayed sign. The bread was supposed to be a free bonus for an album purchase, however, unable to bear to ask the poor old lady to pay, she gave away the bread to her with only a handshake.

The news spread, and as more and more bread was given away for free to the impoverished citizens, a rumor of a Lateran Church charity event spread further, and with it, a bigger crowd flocked to the booth, which was caught by a news reporter who was on scene only to cover an event of a no-name idol. The news coverage subsequently greatly improved Archetto's popularity as a public personality and a nun idol. This allowed her to access more resources for her future idol activity, however, Archetto had decided not to renew her contract as she had successfully promoted the monastery in Lungmen. Before she left the scene though, she accepted [MountainDash Logistics](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/MountainDash_Logistics "MountainDash Logistics")'s invitation to a concert held by [Monster Siren Records](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Monster_Siren_Records "Monster Siren Records"). The recording of her idol show in the said concert made its way to Landen Monastery, which gave the clerics new musical-related ideas to promote the monastery.[\[3\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Archetto/Story#cite_note-3)
