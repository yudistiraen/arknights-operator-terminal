**Bukar Bornu** was previously a member of a stoic [Sargonian](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Sargon "Sargon") military family, with his father being a strict and unforgiving general. Bornu deserted his squad during a sudden ambush after he became unable to bear his superior's criticisms of his lax character, and his father punishing him for his love of reading comics. He contracted [Oripathy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oripathy "Oripathy") after escaping into the wilderness, and after joining [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island") to treat his illness, Bornu has since become a member of the [Op Reserve A6](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Op_Reserve_A6 "Op Reserve A6") under the codename "Spot" who utilizes his previous combat experience to serve as a [Defender](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Defender "Defender"). While he feels like he could never become a true hero, like those in his favorite comics, he has become one for his team at R.I., where he found his new home and family. Taciturn and straightforward, Bornu could amusingly be seen as relatively "normal" compared to the other members of A6.

## Plot\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Spot/Story?action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Plot")\]

### Stories of Afternoon\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Spot/Story?action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Stories of Afternoon")\]

Spot is having a training session with Midnight when Orchid and Popukar appear. He comments that Orchid's expression resembles his mother's whenever things go awry for him. They then move to the cafeteria, where Midnight treats them to a meal while answering Spot's earlier question about how he became an operator. Spot listens to his story. After Orchid and Popukar leave, Spot and Midnight continue their conversation, in which Spot asks if he is worried his team members will betray him. Midnight laughs, confident in his ability to read people now. He reveals that his most important trick is to always tell the truth, and Spot remarks on the simplicity of the trick.[\[1\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Spot/Story#cite_note-1)

### Children of Ursus\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Spot/Story?action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Children of Ursus")\]

At the cafeteria, Spot watches as [Gitano](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Gitano "Gitano") helps [Gummy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Gummy "Gummy") figure out where she should bring the food. Gitano's offer to help carry it is rejected much to her disappointment, and Spot notes that Gitano would not be able to lift it anyway.[\[2\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Spot/Story#cite_note-2)

### Midnight: Letter from Home\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Spot/Story?action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Midnight: Letter from Home")\]

On the night of a mission, Spot knocks on the bathroom door, urging [Midnight](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Midnight "Midnight") to hurry up. While Midnight is still showering, his phone rings, and Spot answers it on his behalf. A voice message in Higashinese begins to play. The voice message is paused midway as Midnight leaves the bathroom. When asked by Spot, he answers that he cannot bear to let his partner wait out in the cold. Spot uses Midnight's phone to call [Orchid](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Orchid "Orchid"), who threatens to cut Midnight's overtime pay if he keeps talking nonsense. The three then discuss the details of the mission, which involves rescuing a kidnapped boy from the local gangsters. After the call, Spot asks about the message, but Midnight reassures him in his usual flamboyant manner. Thanks to Midnight's acting, they soon find the missing person, Jeremy, drunk in a bar and angrily venting about how his father forces him to study when all he wants is to make music. After a brief exchange with Jeremy, Midnight reveals his identity as a Rhodes Island Operator. This immediately alarms Jeremy, who assumes something has happened to his mother and rushes out of the bar.

Back at Rhodes Island, they report to Orchid, and Spot mentions that the boy cried after hearing about Midnight's past. Despite Midnight's usual flirtatiousness, Orchid tells him that she and Spot are willing to listen to him. Moved, Midnight offers to hug them, but is immediately told off before he can embrace Spot. Spot recalls the story Midnight told Jeremy, and Midnight continues from where he left off, explaining how he sent money home and received letters from his mother, while his father refused all contact. Spot asks him if he is scared, to which Midnight initially responds with his usual attitude, but then admits that there are problems he is not willing to face. He starts to ramble to Orchid, then spends the night drinking at the pub with Spot. As the sun begins to rise, Midnight invites Spot for another round of drinks, which he declines as he believes that Midnight will be going on a long trip soon. Midnight's terminal plays the message again, and Midnight tells an annoyed Spot in Higashinese that he will see him tomorrow.[\[3\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Spot/Story#cite_note-3)

### Istina: Let's Go, Let's Go\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Spot/Story?action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Istina: Let's Go, Let's Go")\]

On the day of the food festival, Spot arrives late because [Midnight](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Midnight "Midnight") slipped out without waking him up. He murmurs that he should not have stayed up all night reading comics and has now missed the food festival. He catches sight of the [U.S.S.G.](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ursus_Student_Self-Governing_Group "Ursus Student Self-Governing Group") girls and is curious that they are not packing up their booth, then sees a shaved ice stand behind the stairs, where [Magallan](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Magallan "Magallan") is distributing leftover cans. He hears [Zima](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Zima "Zima")'s roar and notes that the legendary Ursus's Roar is awe-inspiring.[\[4\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Spot/Story#cite_note-4)

### Spot: Face of the Hero\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Spot/Story?action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Spot: Face of the Hero")\]

While trying to return a comic, [Popukar](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Popukar "Popukar") ends up discovering a leather-bound book filled with stories about Bornu the soldier, who resembles Spot and even shares his name, Bukar Bornu. Bornu is humiliated by his own father and forced to read his comics to the other soldiers, who grow to despise him over the ordeal. However, he receives recognition after gathering his brigade during an enemy attack, despite planning to desert on that very same day. The ending brings Popukar to tears and Spot enters the room just then. He tells her to find [Orchid](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Orchid "Orchid") as it is almost her bedtime, and reassures her that he isn't mad. He sorts through the pile of comics and returns everything to their places. Taking a look at the leather-bound book, he ponders the idea of being a hero, an idea that he gave up on long ago.

The next day, Op Reserve A6 heads to the cafeteria together for a party. Spot stays quiet when [Midnight](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Midnight "Midnight") nominates himself as the team member with the best battle performance, seemingly distracted by the events of the previous day. Popukar agrees with [Catapult](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Catapult "Catapult") that Spot had performed the best against [Dobermann](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Dobermann "Dobermann"). Midnight chimes in too, but Orchid tells him to shut up, pointing out Spot's terrible mood and causing the Reproba to realize that Popukar had told them about the comic. Noticing the sullen air, Popukar apologizes, to which Orchid tells her not to bring up the topic while Spot once again reassures her that he is not mad, just unsure how to talk about it. Spot's comparison of Midnight having a brain full of water confuses Popukar even more, and Spot promises to explain later. Orchid looks concerned when Popukar asks if she can tell everyone about Spot's story, but Spot reassures her yet again.

In the cafeteria, Spot shares his past exploits with his teammates. He talks about why the comic ends with him earning a medal and adds that his lousy imagination could not conjure up more heroics for the contradictory idea. Popukar becomes confused and asks Spot if she did something wrong by making him talk about his past, but Spot thanks her. His awkwardness then prompts her to ask if he is sick, although Midnight tells her that he is simply embarrassed to say words of admiration for them. Spot admits that they are the people he trusts the most, and they have a toast for him. Popukar insists that he is a hero and claims she will hold a medal ceremony for him, inviting the team members and her friends. They spend hours trying to talk her out of the idea.

Midnight later visits Spot, bringing him a hero certificate made by Popukar that they can all sign. The signatures on the certificate warm his heart, but he does not sign it and instead frames it on his nightstand. Midnight chides him, to which Spot comments that he is just worried about Orchid complaining if Popukar makes a fuss about the unsigned certificate, and admits that he is no hero. The confession silences Midnight for a moment, but his boast of being overwhelmed at successfully melting Spot's heart makes Spot change his mind. He claims that he can be A6’s hero by ensuring Midnight gets beaten up due to flirting with a bad-tempered girl during their next mission. The two share a laugh.[\[5\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Spot/Story#cite_note-5)
