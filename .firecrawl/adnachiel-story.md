The [Lateran](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lateran "Lateran") [Sankta](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Sankta "Sankta") Adnachiel, a former freelancer, joined Rhodes Island to receive treatment for his [Oripathy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oripathy "Oripathy") after he and [Steward](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Steward "Steward") were rescued by [Merry](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Cardigan "Cardigan") during a snowstorm in [Leithanien](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Leithanien "Leithanien"). Adnachiel is known for being friendly, humble, honest, and intelligent. He is currently serving as a marksman and as a member of [Op Reserve A4](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Op_Reserve_A4 "Op Reserve A4"). Unlike other Sankta combatants who use firearms, Adnachiel has yet to be permitted to use one and instead uses a crossbow in combat. According to Adnachiel, Sankta like him appear from time to time and their minds work a little differently from the ordinary Sankta. In order to win the right to carry a gun, they must agree to join the Curia and be bound by it.[\[1\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Adnachiel/Story#cite_note-oprec-1-1)

### Adnachiel: Reserve Prophet\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Adnachiel/Story?action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Adnachiel: Reserve Prophet")\]

A commotion breaks out when a [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island") patient attempts to see his old house, only to be provoked by the current owner. Adnachiel mediates as the police step in to arrest the [Infected](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Infected "Infected"), but he is detained along with the patients. Later, Adamant, the director of the Ashford branch office, visits Adnachiel in prison. They discuss the plan to relocate the patients to an undeveloped plate in Ashford, noting that the other two counties have already rejected them. However, the recent commotion has now endangered the deal. Adnachiel asks Adamant to find his teammates and relay his plan to them.

First, he has [Melantha](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Melantha "Melantha") play the part of a wealthy businesswoman from Londinium. Her role is to distract the main provocator by offering to buy his house, neutralizing him as a threat. Then, he has [Steward](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Steward "Steward") influence public opinion by spreading the idea of containing the Infected on the undeveloped plate and letting them develop the land for Ashford. He considers it an unpleasant thought, but a useful one to prevent public opinion from forcing the nobles' hand. Lastly, to influence the nobles' decision, he sends [Cardigan](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Cardigan "Cardigan") to the other branches in County Dovel and Hafilax. Her success serves as proof that the other branches will provide manpower and resources for the construction of a new hospital in Ashford. He reasons that although the Viscount will not like it, she will accept the deal as Ashford has nothing to lose, a method he learned from observing the [Doctor](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Doctor "Doctor").

Adnachiel exclaims he was about to die of fright when his team visits and Cardigan calls his name, with Steward commenting that he looks nothing like a despairing prisoner. Adnachiel questions the demand to pay for Cardigan's meals for a whole week, but Cardigan has Melantha help pressure him so he can't escape the promise. He then mourns the naive Cardigan he used to know, to which she claims she has become harder to fool thanks to a certain prophet. [Ansel](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ansel "Ansel") updates him that everything has gone according to plan, and Adnachiel thanks them for their work. Just before they leave, a Lateran messenger steps in, prompting Adnachiel to request temporary leave from Melantha.

The messenger brings word from Cardinal [Lemuen](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lemuen "Lemuen") that the Seventh Tribunal has approved Adnachiel's request to return to Laterano. Steward asks him if he will have a gun when he comes back, but Adnachiel explains that Sankta with the power of the prophet like him must join the Curia to carry a firearm, and he is not returning for a right he has already renounced. As Cardigan says that reserve operator teams are open to those who uphold Rhodes Island's mission, Adnachiel adds that he simply wants to understand what is happening in Terra and promises to come back. Steward, Melantha, and Cardigan find his seriousness strange, while Ansel thinks it is cool, causing Adnachiel to regret saying so much. At Cardigan's request, he promises to bring back new Lateran desserts. He then waves them goodbye and leaves with the Lateran messenger, their parting free from sorrow.[\[1\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Adnachiel/Story#cite_note-oprec-1-1)

### Rewinding Breeze\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Adnachiel/Story?action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Rewinding Breeze")\]

The craftsmen of Workshop No.3 are in the middle of a weapon repair contest when [Arene](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Arene "Arene") comes in and meets Adnachiel. He tells Arene that two craftsmen must inspect his weapon for the weapon reliability certification, to which Arene tells him that the two of them can do the signing. Although amused, he tells Arene not to underestimate the people at Rhodes Island. The inflexibility irks Arene, and he requests that Adnachiel inspect his staff. He agrees but reminds him not to touch anything in the workshop. The mention of his Infected status alerts Arene, although Adnachiel says it isn’t hard to tell. They introduce themselves while discussing Arene's staff. For the last part of the inspection, Arene activates his disintegrative and toxic Arts through a transparent cabinet. They have a chat about the craftsmen of the workshop, such as Iron Hammer who handles staves, and Gunject who only repairs swords despite being Lateran. Unknown to Adnachiel, Arene takes the chance to falsify their signatures. Adnachiel confesses he did not live in Laterano for long due to his abnormal halo and Oripathy. Arene calls him weird, to which Adnachiel defends himself by saying he can't wear a hat like other Sankta, causing Arene to burst into laughter. Adnachiel adds he could not get his firearm qualifications either but he has a sweet tooth unlike Arene, who concludes that there are still ways to reminisce about their home country even if they are forbidden to return to Laterano.

[Ambriel](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ambriel "Ambriel") interrupts, saying that some people like to revisit places to cure homesickness, and asks Adnachiel to take care of her gun. She complains about [Executorfrom](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Executor "Executor") the Notarial Hall who keeps asking her to fulfill her obligations. Adnachiel returns with Arene’s staff and goes to inspect Ambriel’s gun. After Ambriel leaves with her rifle, Adnachiel asks Arene if he wants to get lunch together, but he already has plans with Executor and muses whether he should tip him off about Ambriel, a joke that Adnachiel instantly sees through. Adnachiel reminds him to be more careful as not everyone can tell when he is joking.[\[2\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Adnachiel/Story#cite_note-2)
