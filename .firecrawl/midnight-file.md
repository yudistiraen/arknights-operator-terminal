| + **Archive File 1** <br>Have at least 50% Trust with Midnight |
| --- |
| A former host now assigned to Op Reserve Team A6.<br>Midnight was the most popular producer at a prominent host club in the [Far East](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Far_East "Far East"). According to his own words, he was very quick to retire after his diagnosis.<br>After retiring, he found Rhodes Island through prior connections and came for treatment.<br>Then, after being in the program for a while, he made a peculiar request: He wanted work at Rhodes Island. |

| + **Archive File 3** <br>Have at least 150% Trust with Midnight |
