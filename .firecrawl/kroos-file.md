| − **Profile** |
| --- |
| Originally from [Rim Billiton](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rim_Billiton "Rim Billiton"). Kroos also received her training from the [Columbian](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Columbia "Columbia") Garrison at Holmegard with [Fang](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Fang "Fang") and [Beagle](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Beagle "Beagle"). She is calm and constantly smiling as she maintains a gentle demeanor. However, she sometimes breaks her facade, revealing a sharp-tongued, ferocious side. |

| + **Clinical Analysis** <br>Have at least 25% Trust with Kroos (the Keen Glint) |
| --- |
| Imaging tests have shown the outlines of her internal organs to be indistinct due to abnormal shadows. [Originium](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Originium "Originium") granules have been detected in her circulatory system, confirming her to be infected with [Oripathy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oripathy "Oripathy").<br>**\[Cell-Originium Assimilation\]** 8%<br>She presents no obvious external symptoms of Oripathy.<br>**\[Blood Originium-Crystal Density\]** 0.23u/L<br>Degree of infection is minor and physical symptoms are not yet present. |

| + **Archive File 1** <br>Have at least 50% Trust with Kroos (the Keen Glint) |
| --- |
| Kroos prefers to put in the absolute minimum effort in life and on the battlefield, which is reflective of her weapon of choice - a modern crossbow. Despite her lazy work ethic, Kroos has become a skilled marksman and assists her fellow [Operators](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Operator "Operator") by flanking the enemy and shooting them in the rear with deadly precision. |

| + **Archive File 2** <br>Have at least 100% Trust with Kroos (the Keen Glint) |
| --- |
| Ever since she was a child, Kroos lacked initiative. But during training, she showed an exceptional talent for marksmanship. On the battlefield, she effortlessly locates ideal sniper nests and rains down a hail of precise and deadly fire while backed up by her teammates. Both Fang and Beagle trust Kroos with their lives and she is their support sniper of choice. |

| + **Archive File 3** <br>Have at least 150% Trust with Kroos (the Keen Glint) |
| --- |
| While a member of the garrison, Kroos faced everything with a smile. Upon being infected with Oripathy and suffering discrimination, she had a complete change in mood. After angrily cursing out the other members of the garrison, she along with Fang and Beagle left the garrison for good. In order to find a way for the three of them to survive, Kroos searched far and wide before finally discovering their new home at Rhodes Island. |

| + **Archive File 4** <br>Have 200% Trust with Kroos (the Keen Glint) |
| --- |
| The always smiling Kroos often leaves others with an affectionate impression but does so in order to avoid trouble. She enjoys finding quiet places to slack off and take naps. Although she is typically well-mannered and aloof, when something she cares about is on the line her persona shifts into a more ferocious persona. Even when she is talking, she occasionally displays hints of that ferocity. This facade works on Fang but not Beagle as Kroos cannot withstand her piercing gaze. |
