A [Blacksteel](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Blacksteel "Blacksteel") trainee Operator, the [Vouivre](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Vouivre "Vouivre") codenamed Vanilla was assigned to [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island") as part of their partnership, where she now fights on the frontlines with her halberd. Outside the battlefield, Vanilla is better known as an ardent pet keeper who raises various [Originium slugs](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Originium_slug "Originium slug"). She has a trio of slugs named Puffball, Tiny, and Kuro, plus a [Sargon](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Sargon "Sargon") Desert [Metal Crab](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Metal_Crab "Metal Crab") named Stout. Her hometown in Sargon was a poor place with few resources where children were raised to be great hunters. Vanilla eventually joined Blacksteel as a mercenary to earn money. Vanilla respects her Blacksteel seniors, [Liskarm](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Liskarm "Liskarm") and [Franka](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Franka "Franka"), and is also friends with [Jessica](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Jessica "Jessica").

## Plot\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Vanilla/Story?action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Plot")\]

### Stories of Afternoon\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Vanilla/Story?action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Stories of Afternoon")\]

The Doctor finds Vanilla in the training grounds, having just finished a session. She asks them to help take care of her pet Originium slugs and a desert metal crab. Vanilla is horrified when the Doctor suggests eating them, before suggesting she simply ask anyone she comes across for help. Vanilla finds [Melantha](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Melantha "Melantha") and [Steward](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Steward "Steward"), and Steward agrees to help in Melantha's stead. Their conversation then shifts to Franka teaching swordsmanship to Melantha, with Vanilla expressing slight worry that her senior might corrupt the timid girl. Shortly after, Melantha excuses herself for a checkup with [Ansel](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ansel "Ansel").

Steward and Vanilla talk about how Melantha's mood has improved recently, and then about the cooperation between Blacksteel and Rhodes Island, with Vanilla expressing her gratitude for being able to live a fulfilling life. [Blaze](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Blaze "Blaze") joins the conversation, appearing with Dora, a lively little girl who was recently admitted to the ship for treatment. Looking at the uncommon pair, Blaze jokes about holding a mixer at Rhodes Island. Their conversation reveals that HR classifies Rhodes Islanders in many ways, with combatants making up the smallest portion. Even Dora, whose family Vanilla recalls did not look wealthy, can help by doing simple tasks like making paper flowers for the mixer Blaze announces will be held that day. She then mentions that some people call Rhodes Island a sweatshop, to which Vanilla quickly insists it is only a joke. A call from [Amiya](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Amiya "Amiya") soon pulls Blaze away.

In Vanilla's room, she explains to the stupefied Steward that her pets are well-behaved but will explode if their spikes are pressed, and she shows him the automatic feeder. She then reminisces about the harsh life in her hometown, which drove her to become a mercenary for money. In turn, Steward tells her about his hometown in Kjerag, and how her request to have tea together had made Melantha happy. Before leaving for her mission, Vanilla asks him to bring her along the next time Reserve Op Team A4 visits the children, so they can learn how to make paper flowers for the mixer together.[\[1\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Vanilla/Story#cite_note-1)
