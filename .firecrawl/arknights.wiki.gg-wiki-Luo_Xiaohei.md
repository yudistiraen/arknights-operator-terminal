**[Luo Xiaohei](https://luoxiaohei.fandom.com/wiki/Luo_Xiaohei "fandom:luoxiaohei:Luo Xiaohei")** is a crossover [4★](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/4-star "4-star") [Yanese](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Yan "Yan") [Lord Guard](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lord_Guard "Lord Guard") [Operator](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Operator "Operator") in _[Arknights](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Arknights "Arknights")_. He is the titular protagonist of _[The Legend of Luo Xiaohei](https://luoxiaohei.fandom.com/wiki/The_Legend_of_Luo_Xiaohei "fandom:luoxiaohei:The Legend of Luo Xiaohei")_ who is introduced in [It's Been A While](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/It%27s_Been_A_While "It's Been A While").
