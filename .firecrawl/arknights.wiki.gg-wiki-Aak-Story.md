The [Feline](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Feline "Feline") underground doctor codenamed "Aak" was the son of a renowned doctor. However, an insidious plot against his father following a clinical incident forced the family to flee underground. His father tragically died during a gang fight some time later. Aak then decided to become a doctor himself, but he became a notorious gangster doctor instead. After another gang fight, he was recommended by [Ko Hei-sing, a.k.a. "Hung"](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Hung "Hung") to join [Lee's Detective Agency](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lee%27s_Detective_Agency "Lee's Detective Agency").

As part of the Agency's partnership with [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island"), Aak has been assisting R.I.'s Medical Department as a medical researcher. Although his methods may be extreme and questionable, they have proven to be effective, even exceeding his father's. In addition, he occasionally provides field support, utilizing powerful combat stimulants to enhance his allies' performance, albeit with some "side effects." His boosters are useful. Really.

### Ancient Forge\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Aak/Story?action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Ancient Forge")\]

Aak appears in the _Ancient Forge_ movie as "Back-Alley Doctor." When [Waai Fu](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Waai_Fu "Waai Fu") expresses confusion about the terracotta soldiers, Aak corrects her that they are more akin to ghosts than people. He becomes confused when he and [Hung](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Hung "Hung") are dragged away by a spooked Waai Fu. The three are warned by an officer, but Aak points out his broken arm and spouts about his family line of doctors before treating the officer's injury and giving him a watermelon-flavored experimental drug. He calls the pain relief a placebo effect and instructs him to get formal treatment later, asserting that it is an emergency when Hung calls him out for admitting to being an informal doctor. Aak then wishes the officer safe travels while telling him not to report his assistance, then writes down the drug's performance. Although he is bewildered by Waai Fu's insistence that she cannot fight ghosts, despite having just beaten them a minute ago, Aak decides to leave the matter be so that he can treat more patients. Aak expresses curiosity about the Nian, even wanting to sneak into the containment zone, then spins a bold tale that Waai Fu might meet her dad after defeating the beast. Seeing Waai Fu actually running off to find the beast, he shouts at Hung to stop her.[\[1\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Aak/Story#cite_note-1)

### Stories of Afternoon\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Aak/Story?action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Stories of Afternoon")\]

A few entries in [Ptilopsis](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ptilopsis "Ptilopsis")'s diary mention the day [Aak](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Aak "Aak") and [Hung](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Hung "Hung") first arrive at Rhodes Island. Despite his background as a back-alley doctor in Lungmen, Aak refuses to join the medical department, and he quickly earns a poor reputation due to his obvious disrespect for the medical staff. After discovering Warfarin's identity as Mr. Blood, however, he begins pestering her frequently. One entry describes their second duel, in which Warfarin calls Aak obnoxious for refusing to accept his loss. He then persistently asks Silence and Ptilopsis to judge the result, until Ptilopsis notes that their match, objectively speaking, has no clear victor. This prompts Warfarin to decide to give Aak a loss he will remember. It is noted that the medical department gradually grows accustomed to him as his antics are curbed over time.[\[2\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Aak/Story#cite_note-2)
