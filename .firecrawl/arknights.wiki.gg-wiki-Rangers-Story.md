Don't underestimate this albino [Savra](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Savra "Savra"); nicknamed "the Scimitar of the Bloody Valley", the old man was once a member of the [Sargonian](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Sargon "Sargon") rangers who battled against the powerful lords. He carries the name of his organization and has a wealth of combat experience learnt from his past. As a skilled archer, Rangers joined [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island") as part of [Op A4](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Op_A4 "Op A4"), a comrade-in-arms to [Durin](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Durin "Durin"), [Noir Corne](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Noir_Corne "Noir Corne"), and [Yato](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Yato "Yato"). Outside the battlefield, he will share his adventurous stories to the younger generation so that the legacy of "the Rangers" shall be carried on.
