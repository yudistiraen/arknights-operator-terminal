The compassionate young [Rim Billitoner](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rim_Billiton "Rim Billiton") [Cautus](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Cautus "Cautus") Ansel decided to study medicine for the sake of helping people after his parents passed away, having lost his mother to lung disease, and his father to overwork. As the second-youngest of his siblings, he was sent to live with his father's older brother, while his younger sister was sent to their father's younger brother. Meanwhile, their older brothers were already old enough to work. His uncle and brothers opposed his choice, with his uncle preferring that Ansel work in the company he works for as well. However, with his sister's support, Ansel persevered, intent on studying medicine and finally going to Rhodes Island.[\[1\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ansel/Story#cite_note-rb-ansel-1)

Beginning as a medical intern in [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island"), he gradually became a member of [Op Reserve Team A4](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Op_Reserve_Team_A4 "Op Reserve Team A4"), where he currently works as a field medic.

### Stories of Afternoon\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ansel/Story?action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Stories of Afternoon")\]

In one entry of Ptilopsis's diary, Ansel stumbles upon his colleagues discussing [Aak](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Aak "Aak") and is asked for his opinion on the troublemaker. He replies that he does not connect well with Aak's personality but notes that having a fresh and skilled face around is nice. He then hands [Silence](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Silence "Silence") a report for the experiment.

In another entry, Ansel has a talk with [Ptilopsis](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ptilopsis "Ptilopsis"), listening as she reveals details such as her status as both a patient and a researcher at Rhine Lab. Ansel remarks that he always thought of Rhine Lab as a technology company, then tells her about his past in Rim Billiton and how he joined Rhodes Island, prompting her to search her internal database for her own past. To Ansel's surprise, she claims that not seeing her family is not difficult and then urges him to continue sharing stories about his hometown.[\[2\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ansel/Story#cite_note-2)

### Rewinding Breeze\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ansel/Story?action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Rewinding Breeze")\]

Ansel asks a Messenger heading to Rim Billiton to deliver letters to his family. The Messenger asks Ansel about taking a trip back home to [Rim Billiton](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rim_Billiton "Rim Billiton") instead of simply sending letters, which Ansel promises to do when he has free time. The Messenger leaves to get a box of peppermint candies for the delivery and [April](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/April "April"), who had overheard them, strikes a conversation with Ansel. They introduce themselves and talk about the Messengers aboard Rhodes Island, with Ansel telling her of how Messengers work with Rhodes Island and Oripathy treatment in Rim Billiton. The conversation shifts to the topic of their hometowns, April being from Iron Carrot and Ansel from Iron Fist. April asks why he looked sad when he was sending his letter. He offers to buy her lunch, stating that it was a long story.

Ansel confesses that he does not want to return home as his family, except for his sister, are unsupportive of his choice to become a doctor. His parents had passed away when he and his younger sister were young, so he was sent to one uncle, while his sister was sent to another uncle. His older brothers are opposed to his choice, while his uncle hopes for him to continue his work as a manager in a small company, perceiving it to be a much stabler life than studying medicine. He can only resolve to work harder. April reminisces of the hard but good times she had in Rim Billiton, while Ansel ponders about other choices he could have taken in life. He notes that even someone like the [Doctor](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Doctor "Doctor"), who suffers from amnesia, is still stuck with the past. The conversation ends on a hopeful note about their pasts, with Ansel noting that they were still young and have plenty of time to figure things out.[\[1\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ansel/Story#cite_note-rb-ansel-1)

### Adnachiel: Reserve Prophet\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ansel/Story?action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Adnachiel: Reserve Prophet")\]

At [Cardigan](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Cardigan "Cardigan")'s success, Ansel remarks that she is the fastest in the team and that the other branches must have approved of [Adnachiel](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Adnachiel "Adnachiel")'s plan. Cardigan claims that they only trust Adnachiel because they have yet to see the evil underneath him, which Ansel comments is simply him tricking her into paying his bills once in a while, and she swears to make him pay for her meals for a full week. This prompts Ansel to pity the state of Adnachiel's wallet. The team later visits Adnachiel in prison. Ansel updates him that everything has gone according to plan and admits that he got the easy part of caring for patients. Just before they leave, a Lateran messenger arrives. After Adnachiel explains his reasons for returning to Laterano, Ansel is the only one who finds his seriousness cool rather than strange.[\[3\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ansel/Story#cite_note-3)
