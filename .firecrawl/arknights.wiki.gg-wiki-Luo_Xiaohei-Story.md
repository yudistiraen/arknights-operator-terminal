Some _[Arknights](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Arknights "Arknights")_ Global players may be aware of (or even better, have watched) the Chinese mini-webtoon series _[The Legend of Luo Xiaohei](https://luoxiaohei.fandom.com/wiki/The_Legend_of_Luo_Xiaohei)_ which is seen as an icon of the Chinese ACGN community and recently received attention in Japan after the release of its prequel movie _[The Legend of Hei](https://luoxiaohei.fandom.com/wiki/The_Legend_of_Hei)_. The titular protagonist, Luo Xiaohei, now joins _Arknights_ as the very first character capable of shapeshifting himself between a cute cat form and an adorable human form, as well as a formidable fighter using his supernatural might to protect Doctors from harm!

Luo Xiaohei is a cat Spirit who masters powerful space-manipulation ability at a young age such as teleportation and creating a spiritual domain along with his unique trait of splitting his long tail into four independent _chibis_ named Heixiu (黑咻). After his homeland was deforested, Xiaohei was saved by the human immortal [Wuxian](https://luoxiaohei.fandom.com/wiki/Wuxian), his future master who taught him to wield the spiritual power of metal manipulation. On one occasion, Xiaohei was tasked by Wuxian to steal the Shining Pearl from [Laojun](https://luoxiaohei.fandom.com/wiki/Laojun), but he was severely injured by [Diting](https://luoxiaohei.fandom.com/wiki/Diting) while escaping, causing him to lose much of him power and forcing him to be stuck in his cat form for a long time. In the midst of his hunger and misery, the little girl [Luo Xiaobai](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Luo_Xiaobai "Luo Xiaobai") mistook him as a stray cat and brought him under her care before he could return to his human form. And thus starts Xiaohei's slice-of-life stories where he faces many hilarious moments from his deity counterparts to his human and animal companions.

Xiaohei's story in [Terra](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Terra "Terra") is an accidental one. He, Xiaobai, her cousin [Ah Gen](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ah_Gen "Ah Gen"), and his Chamecavy companion [Biu](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Biu "Biu") were on a search for an [Originium Slug](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Originium_Slug "Originium Slug") that accidentally came to their world. However, things went haywire as they were instead transferred to Terra, forcing them to be separated. While Xiaobai and Ah Gen eventually sought help from [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island"), Xiaohei was brought under the care of [Lee's Detective Agency](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lee%27s_Detective_Agency "Lee's Detective Agency") where [Mr. Lee](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lee "Lee") treated him as his new "son". After the gang finally reunited, R.I. is currently sheltering them while helping the kids to find their way home.

### Luo Xiaohei: Sleepwalker Sailing\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Luo_Xiaohei/Story?action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Luo Xiaohei: Sleepwalker Sailing")\]

In his dream, Xiaohei has his vision sealed by his master to train his sensitivity to the presence of metal, but he loses his master and panics. His outburst wakes up [Xiaobai](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Luo_Xiaobai "Luo Xiaobai"), who comforts him and tells him to go back to sleep. Xiaobai ends up falling asleep, so Xiaohei decides to take a walk outside in his cat form. Wondering about his master, as well as Xiaobai and [Ah Gen](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ah_Gen "Ah Gen")'s family, he gets lost. He shifts into his Spirit form upon hearing the sounds of fighting and discovers [Mountain](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Mountain "Mountain") shadowboxing. When Mountain notices his presence, Xiaohei reveals himself and requests a spar, assuming a stance against the [Feline](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Feline "Feline"). After they trade blows, Xiaohei wipes off sweat and remarks on Mountain's incredible hits, admitting that he got lost while taking a walk after he couldn't go back to sleep. When Mountain realizes he has lost a button from his jacket, Xiaohei at first tells him to sew a new one on, but decides to lend his help when he learns the button is special to Mountain. They introduce themselves, and Xiaohei rejects Mountain's offer to carry him on his back in embarrassment.

In the library, Xiaohei finds a game token and tells Mountain that he rarely plays games or reads books. He thanks Mountain after the Feline gives him several children's picture books. They go to the Convalescent Garden next, where Xiaohei marvels at the queen of the night flowers but senses no metal objects. They notice [Flamebringer](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Flamebringer "Flamebringer") in the garden, who scolds Xiaohei when he tries to eat the flowers because of their scent. Xiaohei rebuffs him, saying that they were not interrupting hiswork, and becomes confused by Flamebringer's explanation that he is not working, but awaiting an unparalleled beauty. Nonetheless, Xiaohei concludes that watching the flowers is enough and wonders about Flamebringer's sword skills as the Sarkaz leaves. They move on to the cafeteria while Xiaohei wonders about bringing Ah Gen and Xiaobai to the garden, and are greeted by Matterhorn. Xiaohei fails to sense the button again and insists on starting over, but is disappointed when Mountain wants to send him back to his dorm. He thanks [Matterhorn](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Matterhorn "Matterhorn") when he gives him candy from [Kjerag](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Kjerag "Kjerag").

Walking through the corridors, Xiaohei half-heartedly replies to Mountain's attempts to reassure him. He agrees to Mountain's offer to teach him reading and answers that he has yet to eat the candy. They encounter [Haze](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Haze "Haze"), whom Xiaohei immediately notes has a strange way of speaking. As Haze continues talking, Xiaohei asks Mountain who she is talking to, to which Mountain whispers back that she is talking to her hat. Xiaohei then furrows his brows and senses metal. He asks Haze about the contents of her pocket and uses his power to manipulate metal, discovering Mountain's button. Caught red-handed, Haze confesses to her crime, and Xiaohei scolds her, saying that the button is important to Mountain. Haze apologizes, then hugs and thanks Xiaohei, which surprises him. After Haze leaves, Xiaohei remarks on her weirdness, then asks Mountain why he is not mad. Mountain answers that he feels more gratitude than anger. They continue walking through the corridors, with Xiaohei yawning and reassuring Mountain that he has nothing important to do. He admits to missing his master and ponders Mountain's advice to do something his master would do to feel better.

A Rhodes Island medic passes them, and Xiaohei decides to check their department, curious about their work and wanting to see a friend. Upon seeing the busy medics and [Sussurro](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Sussurro "Sussurro") handling an emergency, Xiaohei decides to return the next day. He calls Sussurro amazing and wonders if he might be able to help people with stone disease in the future. He compares Mountain's explanation of what Operators do to what his master did, helping spirits without a home and hunting down evildoers. Xiaohei says that he would be truly happy and proud to do things like his master. Now in better spirits, Xiaohei returns to his room to sleep. Some time later, he finishes reading the books and goes to get recommendations from Mountain, approaching him in his cat form. Xiaohei reads together with Mountain, but eventually falls asleep on his shoulder.[\[1\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Luo_Xiaohei/Story#cite_note-1)
