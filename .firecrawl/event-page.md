# Event

From Arknights Terra Wiki

[Jump to navigation](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Event#mw-head) [Jump to search](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Event#searchInput)

In _[Arknights](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Arknights "Arknights")_, **events**, officially known as **limited-time events**, act as content releases that further expands _Arknights'_ gameplay and/or setting by introducing new content from operations to [Operators](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Operator "Operator"), enemies, and coverage of subjects that are not elaborated in the [Main Theme](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Main_Theme "Main Theme") (although not always). Events also act as temporary instances where players can claim various rewards through gameplay progressions.

## Overview\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Event?action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Overview")\]

### Major\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Event?action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Major")\]

**Major events** feature their own [operations](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Operation "Operation") and stories/interludes, and are distinct in that they tend to feature much more robust event stores, and introduce new "tilesets" with some exceptions (notably [Operation Originium Dust](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Operation_Originium_Dust "Operation Originium Dust") and [A Walk in the Dust](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/A_Walk_in_the_Dust "A Walk in the Dust") which reuses an identical tileset) as well as new enemies and mechanics. The stories/interludes of major events are always unlocked in sequence, and is tied to the operations themselves; the major event's plot are usually contained within the main operations, though some are also included in the extra operations.

All major events will receive a **rerun** (known in the CN server as **retrospection**) at a later date following the event's release.

As of [A Walk in the Dust](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/A_Walk_in_the_Dust "A Walk in the Dust"), major events are grouped into two categories and their operations become available to play permanently after release, with some restrictions (see [Event operations § Major](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Operations/Event#Major "Operations/Event")).

- [Intermezzi](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Event/Intermezzo "Event/Intermezzo")
- [Side Stories](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Event/Side_Story "Event/Side Story")

### Minor\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Event?action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Minor")\]

- [Story Collections](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Event/Story_Collection "Event/Story Collection")
- **Login events**: These events can be run during or separate from any other event, as they are "non-intrusive." The systems of login events differ from one another, but they are usually defined by giving out rewards for logging in several days in a row, or add temporary additional rewards to daily and weekly missions. The rewards for these events are typically simple materials and an outfit.

### Celebration\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Event?action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: Celebration")\]

There are two different **Celebration** events.

1. In CN, first Celebration events are released in November 1 to commemorate half-year anniversaries; in Global, they are released as an interim between the year's Carnival and Festival events, and labeled **Thank-You Celebration**.

   - Up to [I Portatori dei Velluti](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/I_Portatori_dei_Velluti "I Portatori dei Velluti"), the Global server releases Thank-You Celebration events in late April or early May (except [Il Siracusano](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Il_Siracusano "Il Siracusano") which, for unknown reasons, is released in late May). Starting from [Retracing Our Steps](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Retracing_Our_Steps "Retracing Our Steps"), they are released in mid April.
2. In CN, second Celebration events are released in May 1 to commemorate yearly anniversaries. In Global, they are released as an interim between the current year's Festival event and the next year's Carnival event, and labeled **Arknights Celebration** from [Absolved Will Be the Seekers](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Absolved_Will_Be_the_Seekers "Absolved Will Be the Seekers"); prior to this, second Celebration events are variably labeled **Gift of the Deep Ones** ( [Under Tides](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Under_Tides "Under Tides") and [Stultifera Navis](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Stultifera_Navis "Stultifera Navis")) and **One Giant Leap** ( [Lone Trail](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lone_Trail "Lone Trail")).

   - Up to Absolved Will Be the Seekers, the Global server releases Arknights Celebration events in late October or early November. Starting from [The Masses' Travels](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Masses%27_Travels "The Masses' Travels"), they are released in mid October.

### Festival\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Event?action=edit&section=9 "Edit section: Festival")\]

In CN, **Spring Festival** or simply **Festival** events are released in late January or early February to commemorate the [Chinese New Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_New_Year "wikipedia:Chinese New Year"); in Global, they are released to commemorate half-year anniversaries.

- Up to [Here A People Sows](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Here_A_People_Sows "Here A People Sows"), the Global server releases Festival events in late July or early August. Starting from [Such is the Joy of Our Reunion](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Such_is_the_Joy_of_Our_Reunion "Such is the Joy of Our Reunion"), they are released in Global's half-year anniversary date of July 16/17.

### Carnival\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Event?action=edit&section=10 "Edit section: Carnival")\]

In CN, **Summer Carnival** or simply **Carnival** events are released around August 1 to commemorate the peak of the year's summer holidays; in Global, they are released to commemorate yearly anniversaries in January 16 (the Global server's release date; up until [Ideal City](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ideal_City "Ideal City") they are released in mid January instead).

## Gameplay tags\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Event?action=edit&section=14 "Edit section: Gameplay tags")\]

Since [Dissociative Recombination](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Dissociative_Recombination "Dissociative Recombination") (CN)/ [When Elegies Are Ashes](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/When_Elegies_Are_Ashes "When Elegies Are Ashes") (Global), events other than Story Collections in the newly introduced Score menu now have **gameplay tags** which indicates what to expect in the event's operations.

| Tag | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Beginner** | The event's operations are the least complicated and suitable for new players. |
| **Moderate** | The event's operations are more complicated than Beginner but less complicated than Challenging, and suitable for regular players. |
| **Challenging** | The event's operations are the most complicated and suitable for veteran players. |
| **Special Environment** | The event's operations feature unique environment mechanisms. For more information, see [Operation#Unique mechanics](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Operation#Unique_mechanics "Operation"). |
| **Special Terrain** | The event's operations feature unique terrain. For more information, see [Terrain](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Terrain "Terrain"). |
| **Special Devices** | The event's operations feature deployable devices integral to gameplay. For more information, see [Device (unit type)#Deployable](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Device_(unit_type)#Deployable "Device (unit type)"). |
| **Special Tools** | The event's operations feature static devices integral to gameplay. For more information, see [Device (unit type)#Static](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Device_(unit_type)#Static "Device (unit type)"). |
| **Neutral Units** | The event's operations feature [allied units](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Allied_unit "Allied unit"). |
| **Elemental Injury** | The event's operations feature enemies capable of inflicting [Elemental Injury](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Elemental_Injury "Elemental Injury"). |

## Trivia\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Event?action=edit&section=15 "Edit section: Trivia")\]

![](https://arknights.wiki.gg/images/thumb/SNS_sticker_63.png/75px-SNS_sticker_63.png?70fd87)

Notice

The following content may be out of date, subjective, or incomplete.

Please consider contributing to help maintain accurate and up to date information on the wiki.

- Due to being 8–9 months behind the CN server at launch and ~6 months behind at present, the Global server of _Arknights_ released events—those featuring limited [headhunting banners](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Headhunting_banners "Headhunting banners") in particular—outside their supposed theme by necessity.

  - Since the [1st Anniversary Celebration](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/1st_Anniversary_Celebration "1st Anniversary Celebration"), Global's content release pacing allows CN's Summer Carnival and Spring Festival events to be released as Global's yearly and half-year anniversaries, respectively, and CN's anniversary events to be released between them.
  - Before the scheduling were made more consistent since 2025:
    - Global reschedules the release of at least one event preceding Carnival events from [Dossoles Holiday](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Dossoles_Holiday "Dossoles Holiday") into February or March to make way for the Carnival event as Global's yearly anniversary event.
    - The second Celebration and Carnival events have a gap of 2.5 months while Carnival and first Celebration events have a gap of 3.5 months, unlike CN where events featuring limited banners are consistently 3 months apart.
- It is worth noting that event operations are typically designed to the advantage of the [Operators](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Operator "Operator") introduced by the headhunting banner that runs concurrently with the event. For example, [Passenger](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Passenger "Passenger")'s ability to chain arts damage to close-by targets would be advantageous against [A Walk in the Dust](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/A_Walk_in_the_Dust "A Walk in the Dust")'s various drone summoners, as would [Heavyrain](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Heavyrain "Heavyrain")'s ability to heal and provide [Camouflage](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Camouflage "Camouflage").
