The [Siracusan](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Siracusa "Siracusa") [Vulpo](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Vulpo "Vulpo") **Angelina Ajimu** was once an ordinary schoolgirl from the [nomadic city](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Nomadic_city "Nomadic city") of Florentia. A lively and curious girl who always dreamed of exploring places beyond her hometown. That was until she contracted [Oripathy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oripathy "Oripathy") in an accident where she was scratched by a truck carrying [Originium](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Originium "Originium") explosives.[\[1\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Angelina/Story#cite_note-1) Pressured by her condition and feeling that she would become a burden to her parents, she was forced to leave Florentia behind, but even then she became an easy prey for the _famiglie._ But everything changed when she met [Martina](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Martina "Martina"), a local Messenger who not only helped her evade the mafia, but also allowed her to help delivering correspondence. In the brief time they spent together, Martina taught Angelina a very valuable lesson: _Bad times will always pass, just as the sun always rises._[\[2\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Angelina/Story#cite_note-2)[\[3\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Angelina/Story#cite_note-3)

Subsequently, Angelina was taken to [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island") for treatment. Despite being a rookie, she works as a Messenger, and in her spare time, she trains her tactical anti-gravitic [Originium Arts](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Originium_Arts "Originium Arts")— which she learned from Martina and quickly mastered— making her capable in combat and a reliable field support unit. No matter how dire things might get, the happy-go-lucky Angelina will always look up with a smile.
