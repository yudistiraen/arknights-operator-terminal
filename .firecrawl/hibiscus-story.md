Born to a middle-class [Londinium](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Londinium "Londinium") family and growing up in a multicultural neighborhood, Hibiscus is a [Victorian](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Victoria "Victoria") [Sarkaz](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Sarkaz "Sarkaz") girl with a heart of gold; Kind and hard-working, she likes to take care of the people around her, especially her younger twin sister [Lava](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lava "Lava"). The two joined [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island") together to treat their growing [Oripathy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oripathy "Oripathy"). Her humanitarian desire leads her to become a [medic](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Medic "Medic") of the [Op Reserve Team A1](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Op_Reserve_A1 "Op Reserve A1"). But perhaps, she is more well known for preparing "healthy" diets that are _really_ unacceptable to others, even including her sister. If one does not want to disappoint Hibiscus, one should eat her food.

### Lingering Echoes\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Hibiscus/Story?action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Lingering Echoes")\]

Hibiscus journeys to the city of Vyseheim to look into the strange "recovery" of Infected locals, also known as the 'Serendipitous Infected Prognosis Phenomenon'. Andante of the branch office welcomes her, and she learns about the upcoming farewell concert of [Czerny](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Czerny "Czerny"). Her investigation leads them to watching the auditions, leading them to [Kreide](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Kreide "Kreide") and [Ebenholz](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ebenholz "Ebenholz").[\[1\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Hibiscus/Story#cite_note-1) That same day, she offers to examine Kreide's grandfather, and insists that he receive treatment at the branch office.[\[2\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Hibiscus/Story#cite_note-2) Inquiries into Czerny's health initially prove fruitless as he is evasive, though eventually she receives his medical information from his long-time caretaker and relative, Ursula. Hibiscus encounters an odd Sankta on her investigations, later revealed to be Kreide's teacher, [Arturia](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Virtuosa "Virtuosa"). Her presence combined with a recently-spread prophecy causes her to become the target of an angry mob, but Kreide protects her and Ebenholz disperses the crowd.[\[3\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Hibiscus/Story#cite_note-3) Though still a target for racial discrimination, Hibiscus continues her investigation while Ebenholz, Kreide, and Czerny focus on the upcoming performance. They have dinner together though her healthy cooking seems to have left an impression on everyone else.

[![](https://arknights.wiki.gg/images/thumb/28_i02.png/320px-28_i02.png?9d0349)](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/File:28_i02.png)

[Enlarge](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/File:28_i02.png "Enlarge")

Kreide pushes his way through and protects Hibiscus from the mob.

She discovers the 'false positive' recovery and the role of Kreide and Ebenholz in the phenomenon. Unable to gain answers from Ebenholz, she urges Czerny to cancel the performance out of fear of harming the audience. Her attempt fails as Czerny argues that her concerns are without proof.[\[4\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Hibiscus/Story#cite_note-4)

Ultimately, she helps Czerny and Ebenholz confront Gertrude and is present when the Afterglow Hall's true nature is revealed. She watches as Czerny exerts himself in composing 'Licht und Schatten' and helps him to the concert hall. During the chaos of the performance, she assists in the evacuation of the attendees. In the end, she can only advise a distraught Ebenholz to seal Kreide's body.[\[5\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Hibiscus/Story#cite_note-5)

[Enlarge](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/File:28_i08.png "Enlarge")

At this moment, she chooses to place her faith in the musician's willpower.

In the aftermath of the incident, back in the R.I. landship, Lava scolds her for her recklessness, having learnt of it from Czerny. She tries playing her flute, which she has only used as a wand, and the sound is horrible.[\[6\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Hibiscus/Story#cite_note-6)

### Hibiscus the Purifier: Nameless Flower\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Hibiscus/Story?action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Hibiscus the Purifier: Nameless Flower")\]

### Ebenholz: Lone Lamp\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Hibiscus/Story?action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Ebenholz: Lone Lamp")\]

After the events in Vyseheim, Hibiscus manages to convince [Czerny](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Czerny "Czerny") to go to Rhodes Island for treatment. [Ebenholz](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ebenholz "Ebenholz")'s sudden appearance one day surprises Hibiscus, who scolds him for making them worry. After he converses with Czerny, Hibiscus asks Ebenholz about his plans, but he cannot give her a clear answer due to the nobleman's supervising him. She promises that the Doctor might be able to help and urges him to come to Rhodes Island for treatment once matters are settled. Ebenholz loses his composure when Hibiscus reminds him of [Kreide](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Kreide "Kreide")'s last words and his promise to honor them.

After Ebenholz passes out, Hibiscus is taken from the house along with Ursula and Czerny. Later, the sound of music in the Afterglow draws her and Czerny near the concert hall. She pleads with Ebenholz not to worsen his situation, but he insists on honoring Kreide's wishes and renounces his nobility.[\[7\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Hibiscus/Story#cite_note-7)

### Stories of Afternoon\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Hibiscus/Story?action=edit&section=9 "Edit section: Stories of Afternoon")\]

Hibiscus and [Lava](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lava "Lava") are giving a recently rescued Perro girl a checkup, bribing her with honey biscuits for an oral exam. It is revealed that the two brought her back to Rhodes Island from the wilderness of Leithanien after subduing her, as she had been attacking passersby while searching for food, including their squad. According to the laws of Leithanien, a rogue Infected like her who had somehow learned Arts was bound to be euthanized. Hibiscus praises Lava for communicating with the Perro and bringing honey biscuits for her, much to her sister's embarrassment.

[Beagle](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Beagle "Beagle") barges into the room and reminds Hibiscus and Lava to attend [Dobermann](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Dobermann "Dobermann")'s class, as she had forgotten to get them out of class. Panicked, they entrust the girl to the first person they see, who happens to be the blacksmith, [Vulcan](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Vulcan "Vulcan"). Some time later, Lava and Hibiscus return from their class and are shocked by [Ceobe](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ceobe "Ceobe")'s development.[\[8\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Hibiscus/Story#cite_note-8)

### Lava the Purgatory: All the Same\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Hibiscus/Story?action=edit&section=19 "Edit section: Lava the Purgatory: All the Same")\]

Hibiscus and [Lava](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lava_the_Purgatory "Lava the Purgatory") visit their parents, who reside in a remote village in [Victoria](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Victoria "Victoria"). While sorting through their parents' belongings, Hibiscus reprimands Lava when she suggests tossing out some of it. The two find recruitment notices for various jobs, all crossed out, which Lava complains about. Hibiscus reminds her sister not to bring it up in front of their parents, and insists that they should focus on helping however they can. When Lava goes to borrow chili powder, Hibiscus warns her not to cause trouble with the neighbors. When Lava returns, Hibiscus fusses over her with many questions, all of which are ignored. Lava leaves soon after with a bag of things, much to Hibiscus' confusion.[\[10\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Hibiscus/Story#cite_note-10)
