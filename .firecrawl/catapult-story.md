The young [Kazimierzian](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Kazimierz "Kazimierz") [Kuranta](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Kuranta "Kuranta") **Arleta** originally served in her local noble's private army until she got fed up with his greed and his exploitation of her fellow villagers. Therefore, in an act befitting her rebellious and impulsive nature, she blew up the noble's vault and returned the money to the townspeople by throwing it into the streets. She avoided the guards hunting her and sneaked out of town with the help of her friends and [the Candle Knight](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Viviana "Viviana").[\[1\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Catapult/Story#cite_note-oprec-1-1) After she contracted [Oripathy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oripathy "Oripathy") in the wilderness, she went to [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island") to undergo treatment. She was later assigned to [Op Reserve A6](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Op_Reserve_A6 "Op Reserve A6") under the codename "Catapult" (a nickname that originated from her friends back home), where she acts as a demolitions expert who provides indirect fire support with a grenade launcher. While she has the reputation of a troublemaker and slacker, often complaining about [Dobermann](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Dobermann "Dobermann")'s strict training techniques, Arleta is nevertheless a reliable operator with good morals.

## Plot\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Catapult/Story?action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Plot")\]

### Catapult: Break Free\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Catapult/Story?action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Catapult: Break Free")\]

After bombing an old noble's vault and throwing his treasure all over the place, Catapult hides in her friend Filon's cupboard and avoids capture with the help of another friend, Raz, a knight in the search team. Filon asks her what she was thinking, as she had been part of the old noble's private army, and does not buy her reason of having had enough of the old noble. Catapult decides to sneak into a merchant caravan heading out of town, and tells Filon to pass on her message to the townsfolk; if anyone else tries to squeeze their lives out, then another explosion might just happen again.

Catapult sneaks into the caravan, finding the transport cramped with a distinct smell. She has no idea where to go next and falls asleep, but is jolted awake as the search team arrives to inspect the caravan. Although panicked at first, Catapult grows amused when she learns that the old noble has placed a huge bounty on her. She sneaks out and steals the wallets of the caravan passengers, causing an indignant crowd to distract the search team, and escapes amidst the chaos. Raz catches up to her, having seen through her tricks. Catapult jokes about his new gear, mentioning that the old fart had always wanted to raise a Knightclub.

Much to her surprise, Raz offers to desert and give her a ride. He believes her when she says she blew up the vault simply because she was sick of the old noble, and Catapult admits she was just channeling her revenge. The search team approaches Raz at that moment, and Catapult nearly gets spotted were it not for a traveler, [the Candle Knight](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Viviana "Viviana"), stepping out and pretending to be speaking with Raz. She winks at Catapult and leads the search team away, while Raz tells the stupefied Catapult that he admires her and that she should seize the moment to escape. Alone, Catapult muses about the new favors she has racked up and decides to leave [Kazimierz](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Kazimierz "Kazimierz").[\[1\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Catapult/Story#cite_note-oprec-1-1)

### Catapult: Change\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Catapult/Story?action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Catapult: Change")\]

Catapult returns to her hometown in the outskirts of [Kazimierz](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Kazimierz "Kazimierz"). She tells a Rhodes Island field personnel about how she once scattered gold coins on the street, then decides half a day is plenty of time for her to look around, promising to bring back souvenirs. After encountering her old friend Filon, they exchange updates on their lives and she learns that after she left, the old noble increased the tax rates for two or three months but retired after an investigation. A young master replaced him, returning the tax rate to normal and investing in the town's development, as well as canceling the arrest warrants from the incident. Filon shows Catapult around the town, telling her how the young master had vineyards planted to import grapes to the city, and that he plans to build a large factory for the entire town. They eat at a pub that was rebuilt from the remains of the old noble's vault.

Filon admits that he opened up a shop with his savings, but profits are low since the factory's construction began from the other end. The good thing is that the knight competitions, which started since [the Candle Knight's](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Viviana "Viviana") visit, have become more popular thanks to the young master, and he can make cash from betting. Catapult discovers that Raz, another old friend, is now an independent knight in the arena, having been inspired by the Candle Knight. Filon decides to bet on Raz despite his opponent being a veteran mercenary. Much to Catapult's horror, he tells her that those who end up owing up money after losing their bets can pay back with labor for the young master. Filon starts to lose his mind as the scales tip to Raz's opponent, while Catapult makes a call to her coworker, asking him to drive to the arena.

Catapult prepares her grenade launcher while Filon shouts at Raz not to lose, more worried about his money than his friend's life. Intent on paying back the favor she owes Raz, Catapult aims at his opponent, giving Raz the opportunity to knock him down and win. She lowers her weapon and leaves, ignoring Filon as she approaches the Rhodes Island personnel. Irritated by the events of the day, she tells him they should have dinner and get some souvenirs before leaving the despicable place. Filon, who Catapult calls an acquaintance, runs toward them but predictably turns around to pick up the money that fell from his bag. Catapult does not look back and complains that she is starving.[\[2\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Catapult/Story#cite_note-2)

### Stories of Afternoon\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Catapult/Story?action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Stories of Afternoon")\]

Catapult finds [Orchid](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Orchid "Orchid") and [Popukar](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Popukar "Popukar"), and asks why the two are hugging. She mentions she just finished working out with [Midnight](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Midnight "Midnight"), who is currently training with [Spot](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Spot "Spot"). Catapult laughs when Popukar recalls that Midnight never asked her to work out with him again after she hit him once. Nevertheless, Orchid reminds Catapult not to cause trouble, and Popukar agrees to come to Catapult's room later that night for story time. Catapult then leaves to take a shower.

Later, Catapult interrupts Orchid, who is signing up for fitness training, and exclaims that Midnight is currently sparring with Popukar. When Catapult insists that she hasn't caused any trouble lately and Orchid says she believes her, Catapult remarks that Orchid seems different. She rushes off, eager to see Midnight lose.[\[3\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Catapult/Story#cite_note-3)

### Istina: Let's Go, Let's Go\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Catapult/Story?action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Istina: Let's Go, Let's Go")\]

On the day of the food festival, Catapult and the other operators visit the booth run by the [U.S.S.G.](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ursus_Student_Self-Governing_Group "Ursus Student Self-Governing Group") girls. Catapult tells [Popukar](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Popukar "Popukar") to watch as she throws a pastry into the air and catches it in her mouth. She offers to teach Popukar, but [Orchid](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Orchid "Orchid") scolds her.[\[4\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Catapult/Story#cite_note-4)

### Spot: Face of the Hero\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Catapult/Story?action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Spot: Face of the Hero")\]

Op Reserve A6 heads to the cafeteria together for a party. Catapult laughs and boasts about giving the drills everything she had, but when asked by [Orchid](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Orchid "Orchid"), she admits to only putting in seventy to eighty percent effort. She stays quiet when [Midnight](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Midnight "Midnight") nominates himself as the team member with the best battle performance. Both Catapult and Popukar agree that [Spot](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Spot "Spot") performed the best against Dobermann. Midnight chimes in, but Orchid tells him to shut up, pointing out Spot's terrible mood and causing the [Reproba](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Reproba "Reproba") to realize that Popukar had told them about the comic about Bornu, who is him. Noticing the sullen air, Catapult tries to steer the conversation back to the party. Popukar apologizes to Spot, who reassures her that he is just unsure of how to talk about his past. In the cafeteria, Spot shares his past exploits with his teammates, much to Catapult's amazement. He talks about why the comic ends with him earning a medal and adds that his lousy imagination could not conjure up more heroics for the contradictory idea. Spot then admits that they are the people he trusts the most, which prompts Catapult to propose that they have a toast for him. Popukar insists that he is a hero and claims she will hold a medal ceremony for him, inviting the team members and her friends. Though they spend hours trying to talk her out of the idea, she later makes Spot a hero certificate that they can all sign.[\[5\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Catapult/Story#cite_note-5)
