A [Columbian](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Columbia "Columbia") [Cautus](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Cautus "Cautus") of [Rim Billitoner](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rim_Billiton "Rim Billiton") descent, Kroos was a police cadet in Tkaronto working together with [Fang](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Fang "Fang") and [Beagle](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Beagle "Beagle"). Following a [Catastrophe](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Catastrophe "Catastrophe") titled "Blackwasp" that led to their [Oripathy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oripathy "Oripathy"), Kroos joined [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island") together with her colleagues to escape the discrimination towards the [Infected](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Infected "Infected"). Assigned to [Op Reserve A1](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Op_Reserve_A1 "Op Reserve A1"), Kroos puts her police training to good use in the field as a sharpshooter, and her happy, upbeat, and fun-loving attitude is actually the way for her to cope with her Oripathy.

After years of strenuous training, Kroos become a skilled markswoman on par with R.I.'s veteran sharpshooters. However, by then, Op Reserve A1 had been disbanded, Beagle was W.I.A. during an operation in [Bolívar](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Bol%C3%ADvar "Bolívar"), while Fang was wounded following a friendly fire incident during an operation in Rim Billiton. Consequently, Kroos is no longer the cheerful and jovial Cautus we knew and love; she occasionally becomes somber and stern, and her depression makes it difficult for her to take a peaceful, quiet nap.

### Kroos: Apples\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Kroos/Story?action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Kroos: Apples")\]

One laid-back morning, Kroos slips away from [Fang](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Fang "Fang")'s watchful eye for a short break and runs off when she is spotted by Fang and [Beagle](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Beagle "Beagle"). She encounters [Scene](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Scene "Scene") in the hallway and lies that she is playing hide-and-seek with Fang. Scene helps camouflage her with Lens, though Fang almost discovers her. While enjoying the music that Lens puts on for Scene, Kroos decides to chill for a bit and asks what Scene is doing. Lens answers that Scene is brainstorming ideas for her next piece, prompting Kroos to ask if there is anyone chasing her and forcing her to work. Kroos wishes she could live like Scene, explaining that being a [Cautus](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Cautus "Cautus") is exhausting since they tend to be energetic, while she prefers lazing around. When Lens says that she can be agile when she wants to, Kroos replies that she could die if she messes up during missions, then agrees that she might be lazy because she focuses her energy where it matters. However, she also adds that it might simply be because she does not want to work. To Kroos's confusion, Lens and the other buggies suddenly break down.

Kroos patiently listens to Scene, then goes off to look for [Mayer](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Mayer "Mayer"). The automatic door is broken, so she opens it manually and coughs because Mayer is painting. She steps outside for some clean air, then tells Mayer that Lens has broken down before leading her to Scene. There, they discover that Lens has rebooted and that Kroos misunderstood Scene's words. Kroos offers to walk Scene to [Closure](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Closure "Closure")'s place to fix Lens's freezing issue, then tells Scene that they can take their time. At the end of the day, Kroos returns to her room with muffins for Fang's anniversary as a captain, then goes to the bathroom while the others head to bed.[\[1\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Kroos/Story#cite_note-1)
