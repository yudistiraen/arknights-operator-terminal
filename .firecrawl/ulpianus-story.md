**Ulpianus** is the Third Commander of the [Abyssal Hunters](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Abyssal_Hunters "Abyssal Hunters"), being the oldest member of the group, who was thought to have died during the great assault on [Ishar-mla](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ishar-mla "Ishar-mla") many years ago. In fact, he is _the_ first subject to volunteer to the experiments and head researcher of the Abyssal Hunter program. But unlike subsequent hunters, the [Seaborn](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Seaborn "Seaborn") cells from his body never attempt to extend their influence, nor does his auto-adaptive junction tries to fight back. Instead, both mechanisms appear to coexist in balance.

Ulpianus not only survived the massacre, but uncovered the mystery of the Seaborn. He found himself inside an ancient ruin within the abyss where he witnessed numerous Seaborn ["Genesezoics"](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Firstborn "Firstborn") and saw the moment Ishar-mla's blood fused together with [Skadi](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Skadi "Skadi")'s, turning her into Ishar-mla's vessel. Hence, Ulpianus remains cautious of Skadi and will use any method to prevent her from returning to her homeland out of fear she'd destroy it through Ishar-mla's revival.[\[1\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ulpianus/Story#cite_note-1)

Because of his familiarity with the Hunters, Ulpianus is well aware of their real secret as well as the Aegirian Counsels' corruption by the [Church of the Deep](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Church_of_the_Deep "Church of the Deep"). During the time he was thought to be dead, Ulpianus uncovered more secrets from the insidious Church after breaking into [Cicero](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Cicero "Cicero")'s laboratory. He learnt of the Church's intention to revive Ishar-mla and their ultimate goal of bringing the second Profound Silence onto all of [Terra](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Terra "Terra").[\[2\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ulpianus/Story#cite_note-2) Ulpianus also befriended the legendary [Last Knight](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Last_Knight "The Last Knight") with whom he shares a common goal of defeating the source of the Seaborn.
