| − **Profile** |
| --- |
| Born in [Londinium](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Londinium "Londinium") in [Victoria](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Victoria "Victoria"), Hibiscus is the older twin sister of her fellow [Operator](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Operator "Operator"), [Lava](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Lava "Lava"). After contracting [Oripathy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oripathy "Oripathy"), she came to [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island") for treatment. While undergoing that treatment, she began to dream of helping others. Her passion for caring for others, as well as her hard work, have led Hibiscus to become a Rhodes Island Medic. |

| + **Clinical Analysis** <br>Have at least 25% Trust with Hibiscus (the Purifier) |
| --- |
| Imaging tests have shown the outlines of her internal organs to be indistinct due to abnormal shadows. [Originium](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Originium "Originium") granules have been detected in her circulatory system, confirming her to be infected with [Oripathy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oripathy "Oripathy").<br>**\[Cell-Originium Assimilation\]** 7.4%<br>She presents no obvious external symptoms of Oripathy.<br>**\[Blood Originium-Crystal Density\] 0.2 u/L**<br>**Degree of infection is minor and physical symptoms are not yet present.** |

| + **Originium Arts Rating** |
| --- |
| Though she possess basic [Originium Arts](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Originium_Arts "Originium Arts"), Hibiscus prefers not to utilize her Originium power. Instead she prefers to use her medical skills in order to care for and treat the wounds of her allies. Given her talents in medicine, Hibiscus has become an outstanding Rhodes Island [Medic](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Medic "Medic") Operator. |

| + **Archive File 1** <br>Have at least 50% Trust with Hibiscus (the Purifier) |
| --- |
| Born in Londinium, Hibiscus was popular among her neighbors as a child, and for good reason - she is considerate, hardworking, courteous, modest, and is good at taking care of people. Though it appears to outsiders that Lava does not like to deal with Hibiscus and that the two sisters don't share a strong relationship, Hibiscus knows the truth - that Lava just isn't very good at expressing those kinds of feelings. |

| + **Archive File 2** <br>Have at least 100% Trust with Hibiscus (the Purifier) |
| --- |
| After the two sisters were infected with Oripathy, Hibiscus took Lava and left their parents to come to Rhodes Island. The determined Hibiscus decided to work hard for Rhodes Island for her sister, herself, her parents back home, and all the other infected. |

| + **Archive File 3** <br>Have at least 150% Trust with Hibiscus (the Purifier) |
| --- |
| Because of Lava's obstinance, Hibiscus has cultivated a tough attitude from the time they were little. "This food is good for you. You need to eat it every day!" "I threw all your snacks away because they're bad for you!" "It's 10:30. You need to go to bed!" From a bystanders' perspective, Lava seems to have a rough time dealing with her older sister. |
