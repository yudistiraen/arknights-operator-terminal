| − **Profile** |
| --- |
| Originally a [Sargon](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Sargon "Sargon") soldier, Spot took the initiative and came to [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island") seeking employment himself. After passing the examination battery, he was assigned to [Op Reserve Team A6](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Op_Reserve_A6 "Op Reserve A6"). <br>He has a lot of experience as guard; and serves as the defensive specialist on the team. |

| + **Clinical Analysis** <br>Have at least 25% Trust with Spot |
| --- |
| Imaging test for this [Operator](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Operator "Operator") showed blurry outline of internal organs with visible unusual dark spots. Unusually high concentration of [Originium](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Originium "Originium") particles were present in his circulation, indicating signs of infection and confirming him as [infected](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Infected "Infected") at this stage.<br>**\[Cell-Originium Assimilation\]** 3%<br>Crystallization visible on surface of body, with many symptoms of [Oripathy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oripathy "Oripathy") difficult to spot due to skin and hair color.<br>**\[Blood Originium-Crystal Density\]** 0.30u/L<br>Infection is currently spreading at a constant rate. Regular observation and treatment required. |

| + **Archive File 1** <br>Have at least 50% Trust with Spot |
| --- |
| A member of Op Reserve Team A6 who used to be a Sargon soldier.<br>He rarely speaks and has little reason to. On the rare occasion he does open his mouth, he tends to be brutally direct. Among the ostentatious members of Team A6, he is truly the odd man out. |

| + **Archive File 2** <br>Have at least 100% Trust with Spot |
| --- |
| A fan of superheroes and pulp action, Spot loves to read comics and has an impressive collection.<br>He has filled a whole shelf with his comic books, and other Operators often borrow them from him. |

| + **Archive File 3** <br>Have at least 150% Trust with Spot |
| --- |
| Although Sargon is a developed country with no shortage of modern entertainment in its cities, the Sargonians always seem a bit withdrawn.<br>Spot himself seemingly has no interest in anything other than comics and fashion. Every once in a while, he will ask [Orchid](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Orchid "Orchid") to give him a Sargonian hairstyle. |

| + **Archive File 4** <br>Have 200% Trust with Spot |
| --- |
| Spot's personality is largely the product of a stoic family.<br>Sargonian military families have no patience for laziness. Unwilling to conform to his family's wishes, he became closed off, aggressive, and eventually ran away from home. |
