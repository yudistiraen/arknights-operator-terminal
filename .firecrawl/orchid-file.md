| − **Profile** |
| --- |
| Former editor of a [Columbian](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Columbia "Columbia") fashion magazine, Orchid began treatment at [Rhodes Island](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rhodes_Island "Rhodes Island") following [Oripathy](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Oripathy "Oripathy"). She expressed her desire to become an [Operator](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Operator "Operator"), passed the examination battery and was assigned to [Reserve Op Team A6](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Op_Reserve_A6 "Op Reserve A6") as the squad leader. She manages the team and provides support with her [Arts](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Originium_Arts "Originium Arts") in combat. |

| + **Clinical Analysis** <br>Have at least 25% Trust with (Violet Mizutsune) Orchid |
| --- |
| Imaging test for this Operator showed blurry outline of internal organs with visible unusual dark spots. Unusually high concentration of [Originium](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Originium "Originium") particles were present in her circulation, indicating signs of infection and confirming her as [infected](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Infected "Infected") at this stage.<br>**\[Cell-Originium Assimilation\]** 6%<br>Infection is expected to spread given the current course. Regular observation and management required.<br>**\[Blood Originium-Crystal Density\]** 0.23 u/L<br>Operator Orchid displays conspicuous symptoms, but has cooperated with the treatment plan and has minimized the spread of the infection. |

| + **Archive File 1** <br>Have at least 50% Trust with (Violet Mizutsune) Orchid |
| --- |
| Captain of Reserve Op Team A6, yearning to return to a normal life.<br>Having previously been an office worker, Orchid is one of the most "ordinary" Rhodes Islanders, but finds herself constantly troubled by the more colorful characters on her team. Her title might be captain, but she's more like the squad nanny. |

| + **Archive File 2** <br>Have at least 100% Trust with (Violet Mizutsune) Orchid |
| --- |
| The former fashion magazine editor still has a nose for the trendy. She never bandwagons onto a fad, preferring to keep to her own vision. The way she dresses usually sets the style for the season at Rhodes Island.<br>More importantly, she knows all the tricks to cover up all the superficial symptoms of Oripathy, keeping the Infected looking the same as they ever did, and even bringing out their individual charms--just as she did for herself. She has been a godsend to many Oripathy sufferers, especially the ladies.<br>As such, Miss Orchid has become an icon to some of the girls on the island. |

| + **Archive File 3** <br>Have at least 150% Trust with (Violet Mizutsune) Orchid |
| --- |
| A modern city girl like Orchid would have never volunteered herself for a highly dangerous job like an that of an Operator.<br>But treatment at Rhodes Island isn't free (though there are discounts and payment plans), and she did lose her job. Ultimately, after a battery of exams, she decided to accept the position.<br>Of course, if she knew she was to lead the freaks on A6, she probably would have had second thoughts. |

| + **Archive File 4** <br>Have 200% Trust with (Violet Mizutsune) Orchid |
| --- |
| But when all is said and done, Orchid knows better than anyone that even though A6 is a freak show, they all have their hearts in the right place.<br>Her egotistical but sincere [Midnight](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Midnight "Midnight"), her deadly but well-meaning [Popukar](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Popukar "Popukar"), her icy but caring [Spot](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Spot "Spot"), and... then there's [Catapult](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Catapult "Catapult")... Right, let's not talk about her. In any case, Orchid treasures her squadmates. |
