The youngest in [Reserve Team A6](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Op_Reserve_A6 "Op Reserve A6") with tremendous destructive capabilities thanks to her chainsaw. The young [Rim Billitonian](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Rim_Billiton "Rim Billiton") could have had a good childhood, but she was sold by her parents to a lumber factory and had been working there without education or even safety precautions. She met [Kal'tsit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Kal%27tsit "Kal'tsit") who personally brought her to Rhodes Island for treatment, which [Ansel](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ansel "Ansel") noted to be the first time he saw Kal'tsit visibly upset. Popukar became a member of Op Reserve A6 despite having failed her operator exam. Although she seems like a well-behaved child, Popukar can go berserk and exhibit destructive behavior, to the point that she could overpower the adults.

### Stories of Afternoon\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Popukar/Story?action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Stories of Afternoon")\]

Popukar appears in the logistics department to look for [Orchid](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Orchid "Orchid"), and the two leave. When asked by Orchid about how she joined Rhodes Island, Popukar reveals she worked at a lumberyard before being found by Ansel and Kal'tsit, having been abandoned by her parents before. She expresses confusion when Orchid hugs her. Catapult appears and asks why the two are hugging, mentioning she just finished working out with Midnight, who is currently training with Spot. Popukar then recalls that [Midnight](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Midnight "Midnight") never asked her to work out with him again after she hit him once, which makes Catapult laugh. Popukar also agrees to come to [Catapult](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Catapult "Catapult")'s room later that night for story time. Afterward, Orchid and Popukar find [Spot](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Spot "Spot") and Midnight sparring in the training room.

They then move to the cafeteria, where Midnight treats them to a meal while answering Spot's earlier question about how he became an operator. His story drives Popukar to tears as she is saddened by what he went through. Popukar leaves with Orchid, who tells Popukar not to go to Catapult's room as it is already late, while Popukar expresses relief that Orchid seems happier than before. The two then come across Sora, who introduces herself and asks about Orchid's expertise in fashion design. Later, Popukar spars with Midnight and is implied to be winning.[\[1\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Popukar/Story#cite_note-1)

### Rewinding Breeze\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Popukar/Story?action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Rewinding Breeze")\]

[Suzuran](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Suzuran "Suzuran") tells the legend of Dusk Beauty to [Shamare](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Shamare "Shamare") when [Mousse](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Mousse "Mousse") and [Popukar](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Popukar "Popukar") join the table in the middle of the story. Suzuran proceeds to narrate the [Higashinese](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Higashi "Higashi") legend of Nito-jo, and Popukar at one point expresses her frustration at the brothers' fate. Mousse shares a Victorian fairytale and the unfair ending upsets the listeners. After a moment of silence, Shamare shares the [Siracusan](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Siracusa "Siracusa") tale of the She-wolf. Last is Popukar, telling a tale she heard from [Orchid](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Orchid "Orchid"), about a [Forte](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Forte "Forte") inventor who molded a wax figure to be his bride, only to be imprisoned by an envious king. But after the inventor was set free, he learned that the king and his army died from the melted wax of the bride. The inventor was crowned the next king and brought prosperity to the country, yet he shut himself inside a room to recreate his bride to no avail. The king eventually died of old age, and the kind people buried him in the garden with all his wax figures. She makes the mistake of calling the inventor a wax figure in the beginning, much to her embarrassment and the listeners' confusion. Orchid appears, looking for Popukar as it is bedtime. One by one, the girls bid goodnight and head back to their rooms.[\[2\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Popukar/Story#cite_note-2)

### Popukar: Funtime\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Popukar/Story?action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Popukar: Funtime")\]

Popukar and [Bubble](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Bubble "Bubble") finish watching an episode of _Felitubbies_ and find [Shamare](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Shamare "Shamare") to be fast asleep. Bored, the two discuss what they want to do when they grow up. Bubble plans to return to her tribe and become a great warrior, while Popukar wants to be a superhero who can shoot lasers from her eyes. Popukar promises she will show her bad eye to Bubble once it has recovered. With only half an hour of playtime left, Popukar tries to explain the concept of homework to Bubble, who has never had to do any. Their conversation then shifts to an earlier incident where Bubble lost to [Sideroca](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Sideroca "Sideroca"). Bubble tried to deny by swearing on her Papa's name, but eventually admits she could not defeat the [Forte](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Forte "Forte").

The topic shifts again, this time to their parents. Bubble admits she doesn't remember her Papa's name, while Popukar knows she has parents but recalls [Kal'tsit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Kal%27tsit "Kal'tsit") once telling her she was sculpted out of clay. They then talk about cold people and baddies, concluding that people who are cold are not always bad, using Shamare as an example. Although the older girl looks cold, she takes care of everyone and brings them tasty apples. Popukar says she only remembers bits and pieces of her previous life, so Bubble smacks her on the head to try and help her remember. Meanwhile, Shamare, still asleep, rejects a call on her phone. Unable to play with Popukar's chainsaw, Bubble starts asking questions about Popukar's teammates, though she mistakes them for servants.

They talk about how Shamare likes headpats and also about her tail, but Popukar dissuades Bubble from disturbing the sleeping [Vulpo](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Vulpo "Vulpo") girl, who mumbles about keeping an eye on someone. They have an arm-wrestling match which Popukar wins, and they continue wrestling until the table breaks apart. As they panic and discuss solutions, her eyepatch gets tilted, revealing the black rocks within. Urged by Bubble, she takes off the eyepatch, revealing an eye so bright that Bubble has to turn off the lights. The Originium crystal inside Popukar's right eye emits a flash and then intense heat, suddenly going out of control. The now-awake Shamare tells Popukar to put her eyepatch back on. Once she does, things return to normal. Morte knocks Bubble unconscious, and Shamare reminds Popukar that [Orchid](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Orchid "Orchid") is waiting outside. After Popukar leaves, Shamare lets out a sigh and her phone rings again. This time she answers, informing the unknown caller that Popukar is fine and that she will take Bubble to Medical. Shamare leaves while Morte follows her, dragging Bubble along.[\[3\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Popukar/Story#cite_note-3)

### Istina: Let's Go, Let's Go\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Popukar/Story?action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Istina: Let's Go, Let's Go")\]

On the day of the food festival, Popukar and the other operators visit the booth run by the [U.S.S.G.](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Ursus_Student_Self-Governing_Group "Ursus Student Self-Governing Group") girls. She watches in amazement as [Catapult](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Catapult "Catapult") shows off a trick by throwing a pastry into the air and catching it in her mouth. Catapult offers to teach Popukar, but [Orchid](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Orchid "Orchid") scolds her.[\[4\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Popukar/Story#cite_note-4)

### Spot: Face of the Hero\[ [edit](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Popukar/Story?action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Spot: Face of the Hero")\]

Popukar goes to [Spot](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Spot "Spot")'s room to return his comic and finds the door unlocked. She accidentally knocks over a bookshelf and tries to put things back together, eventually deciding to group them by title. She finds a leather-bound book with no title, the pages slightly yellowed and the comics within hand-drawn rather than printed. The stories are about Bornu the soldier, who resembles Spot and even shares his name, Bukar Bornu. He is humiliated by his own father, but then receives recognition after gathering his brigade during an enemy attack despite planning to desert on that day. The ending brings Popukar to tears and Spot enters the room just then. He tells her to find [Orchid](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Orchid "Orchid") as it is almost her bedtime, and reassures her that he isn't mad.

The next day, Op Reserve A6 heads to the cafeteria together for a party, much to Popukar's excitement. She stays quiet when [Midnight](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Midnight "Midnight") nominates himself as the team member with the best battle performance. Popukar agrees with Catapult that Spot had performed the best against Dobermann. Midnight chimes in too, but Orchid tells him to shut up, pointing out Spot's terrible mood and causing him to realize that Popukar had told them about the comic. Noticing the sullen air, Popukar apologizes, and Orchid tells her not to bring up the topic while Spot once again reassures her that he is not mad, just unsure how to talk about it. Spot's comparison of Midnight having a brain full of water confuses Popukar even more, and Spot promises to explain later. Popukar asks if she can tell everyone about Spot's story and Spot reassures her.

In the cafeteria, Popukar listens as Spot shares his past exploits with his teammates. He talks about why the comic ends with him earning a medal and adds that his lousy imagination could not conjure up more heroics for the contradictory idea. Popukar becomes confused and asks Spot if she did something wrong by making him talk about his past, but Spot thanks her. His awkwardness then prompts her to ask if he is sick, although Midnight tells her that he is simply embarrassed to say words of admiration for them. Spot admits that they are the people he trusts the most, and they have a toast for him. Popukar insists that he is a hero and claims she will hold a medal ceremony for him, inviting the team members and her friends. They spend hours talking her out of the idea, but she comes up with another idea and makes Spot a hero certificate that they can all sign on.[\[5\]](https://arknights.wiki.gg/wiki/Popukar/Story#cite_note-5)
